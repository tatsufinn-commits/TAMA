# 🏛️ MAPÚA MAJOR ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN DEFENSE CHECKLIST (Build T26)
## The Definitive Panel Defense Rubric: 3rd-Year Design 5 & 6 Plates
**Target:** Final Jury Defenses, Institutional/Commercial Projects & Exit Design Panels  
**High-Yield Rank:** 10 / 10  

---

# 1. ZONING & BUILDING ENVELOPE VERIFICATION (PD 1096 NBCP)

* [ ] **Zoning Classification Identified:** Lot classification clearly identified (e.g. C-2 Commercial 2 / R-2 Residential).
* [ ] **Allowable Maximum Building Footprint (AMBF) Computed:**
  $$\text{AMBF} = \text{Total Lot Area} \times \text{PSO} \quad (\text{Table VII.1})$$
* [ ] **Total Open Space within Lot (TOSL) Verified:**
  $$\text{TOSL} = \text{USA (Unpaved Surface Area)} + \text{ISA (Impervious Surface Area)}$$
* [ ] **Total Gross Floor Area (TGFA) Compliant:**
  $$\text{TGFA} \le \text{Total Lot Area} \times \text{FLAR} \quad (\text{Table VII.G.1})$$
* [ ] **Building Height Limit (BHL) & Angular Projection:** Building volume does not breach the $1:1.75$ angular plane projection from the opposite edge of the Road Right-of-Way (RROW).
* [ ] **Off-Street Parking Slots Provided:** Computed per Table VII.5 (e.g. 1 slot per 100 sqm GFA for commercial retail) + required BP 344 accessible parking slots ($3.70\text{m} \times 5.00\text{m}$).

---

# 2. TROPICAL DESIGN & PASSIVE ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEMS

* [ ] **East-West Solar Orientation:** Main long axis of building oriented along East-West axis; major window openings face **North and South** to eliminate direct solar heat gain.
* [ ] **Service Core as Thermal Buffer:** Toilets, stairwells, and elevator shafts positioned along the **West façade** to block harsh afternoon solar radiation.
* [ ] **Passive Cross-Ventilation:** Window openings placed on both windward and leeward walls to capture *Amihan* (Northeast) and *Habagat* (Southwest) prevailing breezes.
* [ ] **Sun-Shading Devices (Brise-Soleil):** Horizontal overhangs provided for North/South glazing; vertical louvers provided for East/West exposures.

---

# 3. LIFE SAFETY & FIRE CODE COMPLIANCE (RA 9514)

* [ ] **Occupant Load Factor Calculated:** Floor areas divided by Section 10.2.5.2 factors ($1.80\text{ sqm/person}$ for classrooms, $9.30\text{ sqm}$ for offices, $2.80\text{ sqm}$ for retail).
* [ ] **Number of Exit Stairways:** Minimum **2 remote exits** provided for occupant loads $\le 500$; 3 exits for $501-1,000$; 4 exits for $>1,000$.
* [ ] **Stairway Clear Width:** Minimum **$1.12\text{m}$ ($1,120\text{mm}$)** clear width for stairs serving $\ge 50$ occupants.
* [ ] **Maximum Travel Distance to Exit:** Does not exceed **$46.00\text{m}$ ($150\text{ft}$)** without sprinklers or **$61.00\text{m}$ ($200\text{ft}$)** with automatic fire sprinklers.
* [ ] **Dead-End Corridors:** No dead-end corridor exceeds **$6.00\text{m}$ ($20\text{ft}$)** (or $15.00\text{m}$ with sprinklers).

---

# 4. UNIVERSAL ACCESSIBILITY COMPLIANCE (BP 344)

* [ ] **Accessible Pedestrian Entrance:** Provided from sidewalk grade with minimum **$1:12$ ($8.33\%$) ramp slope**.
* [ ] **Ramp Landings:** Level landings ($1.50\text{m} \times 1.50\text{m}$) provided every $6.00\text{m}$ of horizontal ramp run.
* [ ] **Dual Handrails:** Continuous handrails installed at **$0.70\text{m}$ and $0.90\text{m}$** heights.
* [ ] **Door Clear Openings:** All public accessible doors provide minimum **$0.80\text{m}$ ($800\text{mm}$)** clear opening with lever handles.
* [ ] **Accessible Toilet Stall:** Minimum **$1.70\text{m} \times 1.80\text{m}$** with $1.50\text{m}$ clear turning radius and $0.80\text{m}$ high grab bars.

---

# 5. STRUCTURAL & MEPFS SYSTEMS INTEGRATION

* [ ] **Structural Column Grid Regularity:** Structural grid is uniform ($6.0\text{m} \times 6.0\text{m}$, $8.0\text{m} \times 8.0\text{m}$, etc.) with continuous load paths down to footings.
* [ ] **Vertical Mechanical / Electrical Shafts:** Continuous shafts provided for plumbing soil stacks, chilled water pipes, and electrical risers.
* [ ] **Central HVAC AHU Rooms:** Dedicated Air Handling Unit (AHU) rooms provided on each floor adjacent to vertical service core.
