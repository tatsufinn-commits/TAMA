## 1. ZONING & BUILDING ENVELOPE (PD 1096 NBCP)

* **Allowable Maximum Building Footprint (AMBF):**
  $$\text{AMBF} = \text{TLA} - \text{TOSL} = \text{TLA} \times \text{PSO}$$
* **Total Open Space within Lot (TOSL):**
  $$\text{TOSL} = \text{USA (Unpaved Surface Area)} + \text{ISA (Impervious Surface Area)}$$
* **Total Gross Floor Area (TGFA):**
  $$\text{TGFA} = \text{TLA} \times \text{FLAR}$$
* **Building Height Limit (BHL Angular Projection):**
  $$\text{Maximum Height Angle} = 1:1.75 \quad (\text{From opposite centerline/side of RROW})$$

## 2. STRUCTURAL MECHANICS & RCD BEAMS (NSCP 2015)

* **Ultimate Factored Gravity Load ($W_u$):**
  $$W_u = 1.2 D + 1.6 L$$
* **Simple Beam Maximum Midspan Bending Moment ($M_u$):**
  $$M_u = \frac{W_u \cdot L^2}{8}$$
* **Simple Beam Maximum Support Shear ($V_u$):**
  $$V_u = \frac{W_u \cdot L}{2}$$
* **RCD Singly Reinforced Moment Capacity ($\phi M_n$):**
  $$\phi M_n = 0.90 \cdot A_s f_y \left(d - \frac{a}{2}\right) \quad \text{where } a = \frac{A_s f_y}{0.85 f'_c b}$$

## 3. ACOUSTICS & HVAC (UTILITIES 3 MEPFS)

* **Sabine Reverberation Time ($RT_{60}$):**
  $$RT_{60} = \frac{0.161 \cdot V}{A} \quad \text{where } A = \sum S_i \alpha_i$$
* **Refrigeration Capacity:**
  $$1\text{ Ton of Refrigeration (TR)} = 12,000\text{ BTU/hr} = 3.517\text{ kW}$$
