# 🔑 ANSWER KEY & DISTRACTOR TRAP ANALYSIS

### Question 1: Correct Answer = **C (2.0% / 20mm per meter)**
* **Citation:** National Plumbing Code of the Philippines Section 704.4.
* **Rationale:** Drainage pipes $\le 100\text{mm}$ diameter require minimum $2\%$ slope ($1/4\text{ in/ft}$).
* **Trap:** Option B ($1\%$) is only permitted for pipes $>100\text{mm}$ diameter.

---

### Question 2: Correct Answer = **B (1 year / 12 months)**
* **Citation:** UAP Doc 301 Section 31.
* **Rationale:** The Contractor’s Guarantee Bond covers latent defects for exactly 1 year from the date of final certificate of acceptance. (Note: Article 1723 of Civil Code covers 15-year engineer/architect structural collapse liability).

---

### Question 3: Correct Answer = **B ($200\text{mm} \times 400\text{mm}$; spaced at $0.60\text{m}$)**
* **Citation:** Building Technology 1 & 2 / NSCP Masonry Section.
* **Rationale:** Standard CHB face is $8\text{ in} \times 16\text{ in}$ ($200\text{mm} \times 400\text{mm}$). Vertical rebar is spaced every 2 to 3 blocks ($400\text{mm}$ to $600\text{mm}$).

---

### Question 4: Correct Answer = **C ($3.5\text{ mm}^2$ / #12 AWG with 20A breaker)**
* **Citation:** Philippine Electrical Code (PEC) Section 2.10.
* **Rationale:** Convenience outlets require $3.5\text{ mm}^2$ (#12) copper wire with $20\text{A}$ overcurrent protection. (Option B is for lighting circuits).

---

### Question 5: Correct Answer = **C (Datum)**
* **Citation:** Ching's *Form, Space, and Order*, Chapter 7.
* **Rationale:** A Datum is a line, plane, or volume that, by its continuity and regularity, serves to gather, measure, and organize a pattern of forms and spaces.
