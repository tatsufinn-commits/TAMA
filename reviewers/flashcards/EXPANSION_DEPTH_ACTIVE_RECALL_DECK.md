### CARD 1 — STATICS
* **FRONT:** For a simply supported beam with a UDL w over span L, what is each support reaction and the maximum moment?
* **BACK:** R = wL/2 each; Mmax = wL²/8 at midspan. `[STATICS-FUNDAMENTALS]`

### CARD 2 — STATICS TRAP
* **FRONT:** A 5 m beam carries a 30 kN point load at 2.0 m from the left support. What is Mmax (not the PL/4 formula)?
* **BACK:** M = (reaction left) × 2.0 = 18 × 2 = **36 kN·m**. PL/4 = 37.5 is the center-load trap. `[STATICS-FUNDAMENTALS Ex.2]`

### CARD 3 — SOM
* **FRONT:** What is axial deformation δ for an axially loaded member?
* **BACK:** δ = PL/(AE). Units: N·mm/(mm²·N/mm²) = mm. `[STRENGTH-OF-MATERIALS]`

### CARD 4 — SOM BENDING
* **FRONT:** What is the bending stress formula and what is c?
* **BACK:** σ = Mc/I; **c = distance to the extreme fiber = h/2** (not full depth). `[STRENGTH-OF-MATERIALS]`

### CARD 5 — TRUSSES
* **FRONT:** In the method of joints, what do you assume about unknown member forces, and what does a negative answer mean?
* **BACK:** Assume **tension** (arrows pulling away from joint); negative result = **compression** (state it, not an error). `[STRENGTH-OF-MATERIALS]`

### CARD 6 — MOMENT DISTRIBUTION
* **FRONT:** Stiffness factor k for a member with far end fixed vs pinned?
* **BACK:** **4EI/L** (fixed far end); **3EI/L** (pinned far end). `[MOMENT-DISTRIBUTION-WORKED-EXAMPLE]`

### CARD 7 — SIDESWAY
* **FRONT:** When does a rigid frame sway, and how is the correction factor k found?
* **BACK:** Sways when geometry/loading/support is unsymmetric; k = R/R′ from the shear equation (horizontal equilibrium of column base shears). `[FRAME-ANALYSIS-WITH-SIDESWAY]`

### CARD 8 — LIGHTING
* **FRONT:** Lumen-method fixture count formula?
* **BACK:** N = (E × A)/(Φ_lamp × CU × LLF); **round UP**. `[MEPFS-WORKED-CALCULATIONS]`

### CARD 9 — ACOUSTICS

### CARD 13 — TROPICAL
* **FRONT:** Primary passive cooling strategy in warm-humid PH?
* **BACK:** **Cross-ventilation + shading** (humidity defeats evaporative cooling; airflow is the mechanism). Orient openings ~90° to prevailing wind. `[TROPICAL-DESIGN-PHILIPPINES]`

### CARD 14 — GREEN BUILDING
* **FRONT:** BERDE vs Philippine Green Building Code — voluntary or mandatory?
* **BACK:** **BERDE = voluntary rating** (PHGBC); **PGBC = mandatory referral code** of PD 1096 (DPWH 2015, thresholds by building type/area — verify). `[PHILIPPINE-GREEN-BUILDING]`

### CARD 15 — CLUP
* **FRONT:** CLUP vs Zoning Ordinance?
* **BACK:** **CLUP = the plan** (vision/land-use, 10–20 yr); **ZO = the regulatory ordinance** derived from it (zones, uses, densities). Building permits rely on the ZO. `[CLUP-AND-ZONING-PROCESS]`

### CARD 16 — CLUP PROCESS
* **FRONT:** Order the 12-step CLUP process (abbreviated).
* **BACK:** Organize → stakeholders → vision → analyze → goals → thrust/strategies → draft → public hearing → adopt → draft ZO → approve ZO → implement/monitor. `[CLUP-AND-ZONING-PROCESS]`

### CARD 17 — CURTAIN WALL
* **FRONT:** Curtain wall: structural or non-structural? What loads does it carry?
* **BACK:** **Non-structural** — carries only its own weight + wind; transfers to frame via anchors; never floor loads. `[BT5-CURTAIN-WALLS]`

### CARD 18 — PRESTRESSING
* **FRONT:** Pre-tensioning vs post-tensioning — timing and transfer mechanism?
* **BACK:** **Pre** = tendons tensioned BEFORE concrete (transfer by **bond**, plant/precast); **Post** = stressed AFTER hardening (transfer by **anchorage/bearing**, site); ducts grouted for bonded systems. `[BT5-CURTAIN-WALLS + BT3-4]`

### CARD 19 — HERITAGE LAW
* **FRONT:** Under RA 10066, what status do works by a National Artist automatically receive?
* **BACK:** **Important Cultural Property (ICP)** — unless declared a National Cultural Treasure. `[RA-10066-HERITAGE-ACT-SUMMARY]`

### CARD 20 — HERITAGE LAW
* **FRONT:** What is PRECUP and is registration mandatory?
* **BACK:** **Philippine Registry of Cultural Property** (§14) — national inventory; **mandatory** for NCTs/landmarks/ICPs/heritage zones; voluntary for private owners. `[RA-10066-HERITAGE-ACT-SUMMARY]`

### CARD 21 — GOTHIC
* **FRONT:** The 3 structural innovations that made Gothic height/light possible (and what they replaced).
* **BACK:** **Pointed arch** (less thrust, variable spans) + **ribbed vault** (replaced barrel/groin) + **flying buttress** (transfers thrust outward → walls open). `[HOA-GOTHIC-PERIOD-MODULE]`
