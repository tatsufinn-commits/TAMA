### CARD 1
* **FRONT:** What is the maximum bending moment ($M_{max}$) formula for a simply supported beam of length $L$ carrying a uniformly distributed load $w$?
* **BACK:** **$M_{max} = \frac{w L^2}{8}$** (occurs at midspan). `[Statics / Structures 1]`

---

### CARD 2
* **FRONT:** What is the maximum midspan bending moment formula for a simply supported beam carrying a single concentrated point load $P$ at center?
* **BACK:** **$M_{max} = \frac{P L}{4}$**. `[Statics / Structures 1]`

---

### CARD 3
* **FRONT:** What is the flexural bending stress formula ($\sigma$) in terms of moment $M$ and section modulus $S$?
* **BACK:** **$\sigma = \frac{M \cdot c}{I} = \frac{M}{S}$**. `[Strength of Materials]`

### CARD 4
* **FRONT:** What is the minimum slope required for horizontal plumbing drainage pipes $\le 100\text{mm}$ ($4\text{ inch}$) diameter under the Plumbing Code?
* **BACK:** **$2.0\%$ ($20\text{mm per meter}$ or $1/4\text{ inch per foot}$)**. `[National Plumbing Code]`
