### CARD 1
* **FRONT:** What is the occupant load factor for classrooms under RA 9514 (Revised Fire Code of the Philippines)?
* **BACK:** **$1.80\text{ m}^2\text{ per person}$** (net area). `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.2]`

---

### CARD 2
* **FRONT:** What is the occupant load factor for concentrated assembly spaces with chairs/auditorium seating?
* **BACK:** **$0.65\text{ m}^2\text{ per person}$** (net area). `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.2]`

---

### CARD 3
* **FRONT:** What is the occupant load factor for unconcentrated assembly spaces (dining tables, restaurants, banquet halls)?
* **BACK:** **$1.40\text{ m}^2\text{ per person}$** (net area). `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.2]`

---

### CARD 4
* **FRONT:** What is the occupant load factor for general administrative business offices under RA 9514?
* **BACK:** **$9.30\text{ m}^2\text{ per person}$** (gross area). `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.2]`

---

### CARD 5
* **FRONT:** What is the minimum clear stair width for an exit stairway serving an occupant load of $\ge 50$ persons under Fire Code?
* **BACK:** **$1,120\text{ mm}$ ($1.12\text{ m}$)**. If $<50$ persons, minimum is **$915\text{ mm}$ ($0.915\text{ m}$)**. `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.4]`

---

### CARD 6
* **FRONT:** What is the egress width capacity factor for stairways in non-sprinklered buildings?
* **BACK:** **$7.6\text{ mm per person}$** ($0.3\text{ in/person}$). Multiplied by occupant load to determine total required stair width. `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.2]`

---

### CARD 7
* **FRONT:** What is the egress width capacity factor for level components, corridors, and exit doors?
* **BACK:** **$5.0\text{ mm per person}$** ($0.2\text{ in/person}$) for non-sprinklered / **$3.8\text{ mm per person}$** with approved automatic sprinklers. `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.2]`

---

### CARD 8
* **FRONT:** What is the maximum allowable travel distance to an exit in a building WITHOUT an automatic fire sprinkler system?
* **BACK:** **$46.00\text{ m}$ ($150\text{ ft}$)**. `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.2]`

---

### CARD 9
* **FRONT:** What is the maximum allowable travel distance to an exit in a building WITH an approved automatic fire sprinkler system?
* **BACK:** **$61.00\text{ m}$ ($200\text{ ft}$)**. `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.2]`

---

### CARD 10
* **FRONT:** What is the maximum allowable dead-end corridor length in non-sprinklered vs sprinklered buildings?
* **BACK:** **$6.00\text{ m}$ ($20\text{ ft}$)** without sprinklers; **$15.00\text{ m}$ ($50\text{ ft}$)** with approved sprinkler system. `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.2]`

---

### CARD 11
* **FRONT:** What is the minimum separation distance between two required exits under the Half-Diagonal Rule?
* **BACK:** At least **$\frac{1}{2}$ the overall diagonal ($D/2$)** for non-sprinklered buildings; reduced to **$\frac{1}{3}$ the overall diagonal ($D/3$)** for sprinklered buildings. Measured in a straight line between exit door edges. `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.2]`

---

### CARD 12
* **FRONT:** How many independent exits are required for occupant loads of (a) $1\text{--}500$, (b) $501\text{--}1,000$, and (c) $>1,000$?
* **BACK:** (a) **Minimum 2 Exits**, (b) **Minimum 3 Exits**, (c) **Minimum 4 Exits**. `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.2]`

---

### CARD 13
* **FRONT:** What is the maximum riser height and minimum tread run for exit stairs under RA 9514?
* **BACK:** Max Riser: **$200\text{ mm}$** (Class A: $190\text{ mm}$); Min Tread: **$250\text{ mm}$** (Class A: $280\text{ mm}$); Min Headroom: **$2,000\text{ mm}$**. `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.4]`

---

### CARD 14
* **FRONT:** When is panic hardware statutorily required on an exit door?
* **BACK:** On latching doors serving **Assembly and Educational occupancies with $\ge 50$ persons** (or high hazard spaces). Must release on **$\le 66\text{ N}$ ($15\text{ lbf}$)** push force. `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.3]`

---

### CARD 15
* **FRONT:** What is the required fire resistance rating (FRR) for vertical stair enclosures in buildings $\ge 4$ storeys?
* **BACK:** **2-Hour Fire Resistance Rating** with **1.5-Hour self-closing fire doors**. (Buildings $\le 3$ storeys require 1-Hour FRR with 45-min doors). `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.5.4]`

---

### CARD 16
* **FRONT:** What is the critical distinction between FSEC and FSIC permits?
* **BACK:** **FSEC** (Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance) is issued *before building permit release* after plan review; **FSIC** (Fire Safety Inspection Certificate) is issued *after physical inspection* for occupancy permits and annual business renewals. `[RA 9514 Sec. 7 & IRR Rule 9]`

---

### CARD 17
* **FRONT:** What building height threshold classifies a structure as a "High-Rise Building" under RA 9514, triggering mandatory AFSS and pressurized stairwells?
* **BACK:** Habitable height of **more than $15.00\text{ m}$** above grade. `[RA 9514 IRR Sec. 10.2.19]`

---

### CARD 18
* **FRONT:** What is the minimum compressive strength ($f'_{ci}$) of concrete required before transferring full prestressing force in post-tensioned members?
* **BACK:** **$20.5\text{ MPa}$ ($3,000\text{ psi}$)**. `[ACI 318 / NSCP 2015 Sec. 418.18]`

---

### CARD 19
* **FRONT:** What is the standard refrigeration cooling capacity equivalent of 1 Ton of Refrigeration (TR)?
* **BACK:** **$12,000\text{ BTU/hr} = 3.517\text{ kW}$**. `[ASHRAE / Utilities 3]`

---

### CARD 20
* **FRONT:** What historic Philippine disaster led directly to the passage of RA 9514 and strict enforcement of outward-swinging doors with panic hardware?
* **BACK:** The **Ozone Disco Club Fire (Quezon City, 1996 - 162 deaths)**, where victims were trapped behind an inward-swinging door with padlocked secondary exits. `[RA 9514 Historical Background]`
