# ⚡ BP 344 & PD 1096 NBCP — ACTIVE RECALL FLASHCARD DECK

---

### CARD 1
* **FRONT:** What is the maximum allowable slope for a pedestrian accessibility ramp under BP 344?
* **BACK:** **1:12 (8.33%)**. A level landing of at least $1.50\text{m}$ length is required every $6.00\text{m}$ of horizontal run. `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 2]`

---

### CARD 2
* **FRONT:** What are the standard heights required for BP 344 dual handrails on ramps and stairs?
* **BACK:** **$0.70\text{m}$ ($700\text{mm}$) and $0.90\text{m}$ ($900\text{mm}$)** above the floor level, with **$0.30\text{m}$ ($300\text{mm}$)** horizontal extensions at top and bottom. `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 2]`

---

### CARD 3
* **FRONT:** What is the minimum clear width required for an accessible door opening under BP 344?
* **BACK:** **$0.80\text{m}$ ($800\text{mm}$)**, measured between the face of the door and the door stop with the door open $90^\circ$. `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 3]`

---

### CARD 4
* **FRONT:** What are the standard dimensions for a standard perpendicular off-street car parking slot under PD 1096?
* **BACK:** **$2.50\text{m} \times 5.00\text{m}$** (Minimum clear width = $2.50\text{m}$). `[PD 1096 NBCP Table VII.5]`

---

### CARD 5
* **FRONT:** What are the standard dimensions for an accessible (PWD) parking slot under BP 344?
* **BACK:** **$3.70\text{m} \times 5.00\text{m}$** (Includes a $2.50\text{m}$ parking bay $+ 1.20\text{m}$ clear transfer aisle). `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 1]`

---

### CARD 6
* **FRONT:** What is the Maximum Allowable Percentage of Site Occupancy (PSO) for an **Inside Lot** in an **R-1** residential zone without a firewall?
* **BACK:** **50%** (The Total Open Space within Lot [TOSL] must be at least 50%). `[PD 1096 NBCP Table VII.1]`

---

### CARD 7
* **FRONT:** What is the Maximum Allowable Percentage of Site Occupancy (PSO) for an **Inside Lot** in a **C-2** commercial zone without a firewall?
* **BACK:** **75%** (The Total Open Space within Lot [TOSL] must be at least 25%). `[PD 1096 NBCP Table VII.1]`

---

### CARD 8
* **FRONT:** What is the minimum clear turning diameter required for a wheelchair in accessible toilet stalls?
* **BACK:** **$1.50\text{m}$ ($1,500\text{mm}$) diameter** turning circle ($360^\circ$ turn). `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 7]`

---

### CARD 9
* **FRONT:** What are the standard internal dimensions for an accessible toilet stall under BP 344?
* **BACK:** Minimum **$1.70\text{m}$ width $\times 1.80\text{m}$ depth** ($1.50\text{m} \times 1.70\text{m}$ absolute minimum), with an **outward-swinging door**. `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 7]`

---

### CARD 10
* **FRONT:** What is the required height from the floor to the top of an accessible water closet seat?
* **BACK:** **$0.45\text{m}$ ($450\text{mm}$)**. `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 7]`

---

### CARD 11
* **FRONT:** At what height must grab bars beside the water closet be installed in accessible toilet stalls?
* **BACK:** **$0.80\text{m}$ ($800\text{mm}$)** above the finished floor line. `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 7]`

---

### CARD 12
* **FRONT:** What is the maximum allowable rim height and minimum knee clearance for an accessible lavatory (wash basin)?
* **BACK:** Max rim height: **$0.80\text{m}$ ($800\text{mm}$)**; Knee clearance under basin: **$0.60\text{m to } 0.70\text{m}$ height** and **$0.50\text{m}$ depth**. `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 7]`

---

### CARD 13
* **FRONT:** What is the maximum allowable threshold height at doors along accessible routes?
* **BACK:** **$25\text{mm}$ ($1\text{ inch}$)**, and edges must be beveled / chamfered at a slope not exceeding $1:2$. `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 3]`

---

### CARD 14
* **FRONT:** What is the preferred mounting height for lever-type door handles under BP 344?
* **BACK:** **$0.82\text{m to } 1.06\text{m}$** above the floor (**$0.90\text{m}$ nominal**). `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 3]`

---

### CARD 15
* **FRONT:** What is the minimum lip height for protective side curbs along accessible ramps?
* **BACK:** **$0.10\text{m}$ ($100\text{mm}$)** to prevent wheelchair wheels and crutches from slipping off. `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 2]`

---

### CARD 16
* **FRONT:** What is the maximum walking distance permitted from an accessible parking slot to the building entrance?
* **BACK:** **$30.00\text{m}$**, over an accessible walkway with maximum slope of $1:50$ ($2\%$). `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 1]`

---

### CARD 17
* **FRONT:** What is the required interval for wheelchair rest stops / passing bays along long corridors?
* **BACK:** A $1.50\text{m} \times 1.50\text{m}$ passing bay must be provided **every $12.00\text{m}$** of walkway length. `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 1]`

---

### CARD 18
* **FRONT:** What are the minimum internal dimensions for an accessible passenger elevator cab under BP 344?
* **BACK:** Minimum clear interior dimensions of **$1.10\text{m}$ width $\times 1.40\text{m}$ depth**, with a minimum door clear opening of **$0.80\text{m}$**. `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 5]`

---

### CARD 19
* **FRONT:** At what height should elevator call buttons and light switches be installed for wheelchair reach?
* **BACK:** Elevator buttons: **$0.90\text{m to } 1.20\text{m}$**; Light switches: **$1.20\text{m to } 1.30\text{m}$** above the floor. `[BP 344 Rule II Sec. 5 & 6]`

---

### CARD 20
* **FRONT:** What is the statutory percentage of living units in government housing accommodations that must be accessible to PWDs under BP 344?
* **BACK:** At least **$10\%$ of all units** must be barrier-free and fully accessible (any fraction $> 1/2$ counts as one additional unit). `[Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 Section 1]`
