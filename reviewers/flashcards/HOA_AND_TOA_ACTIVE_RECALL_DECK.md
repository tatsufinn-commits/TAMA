### CARD 1
* **FRONT:** Who were the principal architects of the Parthenon in Athens, Greece?

### CARD 2
* **FRONT:** What architectural term describes the slight convex curvature applied to Greek Doric columns to correct the optical illusion of concavity?
* **BACK:** **Entasis**. `[HOA 1 / TOA 1]`

### CARD 3
* **FRONT:** What is the defining structural innovation that allowed the Byzantine dome of Hagia Sophia to transition onto a square ground plan?
* **BACK:** **Pendentives** (triangular spherical masonry vaults). `[HOA 2]`

---

### CARD 4
* **FRONT:** What are Le Corbusier’s celebrated "Five Points of Modern Architecture" (exemplified in Villa Savoye)?
* **BACK:** 
  1. **Pilotis** (Reinforced concrete stilts)
  2. **Free Plan** (Load-bearing walls eliminated)
  3. **Free Façade** (Curtain wall skin)
  4. **Ribbon Windows** (Horizontal daylighting)
  5. **Roof Garden** (Restored natural ground on roof) `[HOA 4]`

---

### CARD 5
* **FRONT:** In Francis D.K. Ching's Theory of Architecture, what is the difference between an **Axis** and a **Datum**?
* **BACK:** 
  * An **Axis** is a directional line connecting two points about which spaces are arranged.
  * A **Datum** is a line, plane, or volume that serves to gather, measure, and anchor a pattern of disparate spaces. `[TOA 2]`
