## 1. WOOD DEFECTS & JOINERY TYPES

* **Natural Wood Defects:**
  * **Knots:** Cross-sections of branches embedded in timber; reduces tensile/bending strength.
  * **Shakes:** Separation along the grain between annual growth rings.
  * **Checks:** Cracks extending across growth rings caused by uneven drying.
  * **Warping:** Distortion caused by shrinkage (Bow, Crook, Cup, Twist).
* **Wood Joinery Types:**
  * **Lap Joint:** Simple overlapping of two members; requires mechanical fasteners.
  * **Mortise & Tenon:** One member has a projecting tenon fitted into a mortise cavity; high structural rigidity.
  * **Dovetail Joint:** Trapezoidal interlocking fingers; resists tensile pull-out (drawers, cabinet corners).
  * **Scarf Joint:** Beveled splice joint used to lengthen timber beams horizontally.

## 2. CONCRETE HOLLOW BLOCKS (CHB) & MORTAR

* **Standard Philippine CHB Dimensions:**
  * Thickness: $0.10\text{m}$ ($4\text{ inch}$ - interior non-load bearing) or $0.15\text{m}$ ($6\text{ inch}$ - exterior wall).
  * Length & Height: **$0.20\text{m} \times 0.40\text{m}$ ($200\text{mm} \times 400\text{mm}$)**.
* **Standard Mortar / Grout Mix:**
  * Class A: 1 part cement : 2 parts sand (High-strength waterproofing/columns).
  * Class B: 1 part cement : 3 parts sand (General CHB laying & plastering).
  * Class C: 1 part cement : 4 parts sand (Non-structural fill).
* **Reinforcing Bars in CHB:**
  * Vertical Bars: $10\text{mm}$ or $12\text{mm}$ diameter spaced at **$0.40\text{m}$ to $0.60\text{m}$** on center.
  * Horizontal Bars: Spaced every **$0.60\text{m}$ ($3\text{ layers})$**.
