## 1. CONCRETE HOLLOW BLOCKS (CHB) — the Philippine walling staple

**Standard dimensions (Philippine market):**
- Face: **400 mm (16") × 200 mm (8")** nominal (some markets 406×203).
- Thickness: **100 mm (4")**, **150 mm (6")**, **200 mm (8")**.
- Typical holes: 2–3 cells (4" commonly 2–3 holes).
- **Weight:** 4" ≈ 7–12 kg; 6" ≈ 10–17 kg; 8" ≈ 18–22 kg (varies by source — treat as ranges).

**Typical use:**
| Thickness | Use |
|---|---|
| 4" (100 mm) | Interior partitions, fences, light exterior (non-load-bearing) |
| 6" (150 mm) | Exterior walls, load-bearing infill in confined masonry, perimeter walls |
| 8" (200 mm) | Retaining walls, heavy-duty/foundation-level work (often reinforced) |

**Quality/spec (per PH guides + ASTM C-90 reference):**
- Load-bearing units: Type I Class A or B per ASTM C-90-70 (verify current); min compressive strength ~5 MPa individual / 6 MPa average gross area (as cited in shelter guides — verify against current PNS).
- Manufacturing: avoid sea-sand (corrosion); DTI/DPWH-certified suppliers recommended.

**Estimating (standard PH practice — the exam-useful numbers):**
- With 10 mm mortar joints: each block ≈ 0.41 m × 0.21 m = 0.0861 m² → **~12.5 blocks/m²** theoretical; **~13 blocks/m²** with ~5% waste (market practice).
- Mortar: ~0.5 bags cement/m² (4" wall, 1:3 mix) ; ~0.65 bags/m² (6" wall, thicker joints) — ranges, verify per mix.
- **Trap:** using 12.5 without waste vs 13 with waste — questions specify "theoretical" vs "including waste."

## 2. REINFORCED CONCRETE — the structural core

- Materials: Portland cement (40 kg bags — standard PH retail unit), sand, gravel, rebar (10 mm, 12 mm, 16 mm common; 6 m lengths standard).
- Typical residential: RC frame (columns + beams) with **CHB infill (confined masonry)** — the dominant PH low-rise system.
- **Confined masonry** (PH practice): RC tie-columns + bond beams confining CHB panels — improves seismic/typhoon behavior (vs full RC frame with infill). Key PH-specific structural concept.
- Concrete classes per NSCP/PD 1096 IRR (class A/B/C by mix proportion & use) — verify exact table from IRR RAW text (`PD-1096_IRR-2005_FULLTEXT.txt` has concrete class requirements under Rule XII area — check before citing numbers).

## 3. ROOFING

- **GI corrugated sheets** — dominant affordable roofing; gauge/thickness matters (Gauge 26, 24 typical for residential; heavier for industrial).
- **Long-span metal roofing (prepainted)** — common for commercial/industrial; purlin spacing per manufacturer tables.
- **Traditional:** nipa, cogon, anahaw (thatched) — rural/temporary/vernacular; fire-risk classification under the Fire Code (Class per RA 9514/IRR — verify) — a classic code tie-in.
- **Trap:** GI sheet gauge vs thickness confusion; thatched roofing fire classification (NBCP/RA 9514 restrict or require treatment).

## 4. TRADITIONAL / INDIGENOUS MATERIALS (ties to PH Architecture file)

| Material | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Bamboo (kawayan)** | Framing, walls (sawali/amakan), scaffolding | Cheap, renewable, typhoon-vulnerable if unseasoned; PH Green Building interest |
| **Nipa / cogon / anahaw** | Thatch roofing | Fire hazard class; short lifespan (3–5 yrs typical) |
| **Coconut lumber (yakal-type hardwoods vs coco lumber)** | Framing, formwork | Coco lumber cheap but lower durability; termite risk |
| **Capiz** | Window shells (traditional) | Heritage material (bahay na bato) |
| **Adobe/volcanic tuff (adobe stone)** | Heritage masonry (Intramuros churches) | Local stone; heritage conservation context |
| **Sawali (woven bamboo)** | Wall cladding | Vernacular; also used above CHB in shelter construction |

## 5. PH CONSTRUCTION PRACTICE NOTES

- **Sequencing (low-rise):** site clearing → footing/foundation (RC) → columns/beams (formwork → rebar → pour) → CHB walls → roof framing → finishes (plaster, paint) → MEPFS rough-in coordination.
- **Formwork:** plywood (4'×8') + coco lumber; plywood reuse cycles affect cost.
- **Labor conventions:** mason + laborer day-rates (region-dependent — time-sensitive, don't store as fact).
- **Permits:** building permit (PD 1096) + Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (RA 9514) + locational clearance (zoning) + ECC if applicable (PD 1586) — the complete PH clearance chain (ties to COMMON-MISTAKES L8).
