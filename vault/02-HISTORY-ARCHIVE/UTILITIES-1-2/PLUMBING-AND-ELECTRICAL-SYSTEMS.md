## 1. PLUMBING DRAINAGE, WASTE & VENT (DWV) STANDARDS

* **Standard Slope of Horizontal Drainage Pipes:**
  * Pipes $\le 100\text{mm}$ ($4\text{ inch}$): Minimum **$2\%$ slope** ($20\text{mm per meter}$ or $1/4\text{ inch per foot}$).
  * Pipes $> 100\text{mm}$: May be reduced to **$1\%$ slope** ($10\text{mm per meter}$) with local authority approval.
* **Minimum Pipe Diameters (Plumbing Code):**
  * Water Closet (Toilet): Minimum **$75\text{mm}$ to $100\text{mm}$ ($3\text{ to }4\text{ inch}$)** waste pipe.
  * Lavatory (Sink): Minimum **$32\text{mm}$ to $38\text{mm}$ ($1-1/4\text{ to }1-1/2\text{ inch}$)** waste pipe.
  * Floor Drain / Shower: Minimum **$50\text{mm}$ ($2\text{ inch}$)** waste pipe.
* **Septic Tank Sizing (Rule of Thumb):**
  * Minimum Digestion Chamber: At least $2/3$ of total septic volume.
  * Minimum Leaching Chamber: At least $1/3$ of total septic volume.
  * Minimum liquid depth: $1.20\text{m}$; air space clearance above liquid: $0.30\text{m}$.

## 2. ELECTRICAL BRANCH CIRCUITS (PHILIPPINE ELECTRICAL CODE — PEC)

* **Standard Voltage:** $230\text{V}$, single-phase, 60 Hz (Philippine standard).
* **Wire Sizing & Circuit Breakers:**
  * **Lighting Circuits (General):** $2.0\text{ mm}^2$ (AWG #14) copper wire with **$15\text{A}$** circuit breaker.
  * **Convenience Outlets (Receptacles):** $3.5\text{ mm}^2$ (AWG #12) copper wire with **$20\text{A}$** circuit breaker.
  * **Heavy Appliance / Aircon Unit:** $5.5\text{ mm}^2$ (AWG #10) or larger with dedicated **$30\text{A}$** breaker.
* **Maximum Outlets per Circuit:** Maximum **8 to 10 convenience outlets** per $20\text{A}$ branch circuit.
