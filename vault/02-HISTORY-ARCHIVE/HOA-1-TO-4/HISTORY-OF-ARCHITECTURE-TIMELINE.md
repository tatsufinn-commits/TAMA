## 1. CLASSICAL WESTERN ORDERS (GREEK & ROMAN)

* **Doric Order:** Oldest, simplest; no base; 20 flutes; triglyphs & metopes on frieze. Entasis applied. (Example: *Parthenon, Ictinus & Callicrates*).
* **Ionic Order:** Volute (spiral scroll) capital; fluted column with molded base; continuous frieze. (Example: *Erechtheion, Mnesicles*).
* **Corinthian Order:** Acanthus leaf capital; slender fluted shaft. (Example: *Temple of Olympian Zeus*).
* **Tuscan Order (Roman):** Simplified Doric without fluting; smooth shaft with simple base and capital.
* **Composite Order (Roman):** Hybrid capital combining Ionic volutes with Corinthian acanthus leaves.
