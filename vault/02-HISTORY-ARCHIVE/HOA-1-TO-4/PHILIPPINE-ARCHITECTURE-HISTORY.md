## 1. Philippine Architecture — Period Framework (Period → Context → Characteristics → Works)

### 1.1 Pre-colonial / Indigenous
- **Context:** Austronesian settlement; tropical climate; communal societies; no formal "architect" — building was vernacular craft.
- **Characteristics:** raised floors (stilt), steep roofs, natural permeable materials, climate-responsive; modular bamboo/timber joinery without nails.
- **Key type — Bahay Kubo:** single-room nipa hut; raised on stilts (ventilation/flood); steep thatch roof (rain/heat); woven bamboo (sawali) walls; no corridors (spaces merge).
- **Other types:** Ifugao bale (raised, pyramidal); Maranao torogan (royal house, okir carving); badjao houseboats; granaries (Ifugao alang).
- **Exam angle:** identify features vs Spanish house; climate rationale.

### 1.2 Islamic / Southern Philippines (parallel tradition)
- **Context:** Sultanate polities; trade with SE Asia; animist-Islamic blend.
- **Characteristics:** torogan (Lanao) with panolong (winglike beams); okir/ukkil geometric-floral carving; mosque typology adapting the langgal (prayer house).
- **Exam angle:** distinguish torogan from bahay kubo; okir motifs.

### 1.3 Spanish Colonial (1565–1898)
- **Context:** colonization; Christianization; the friar-architect; labor conscription; local materials + European typologies.
- **Characteristics:**
  - **Bahay na Bato:** stone lower floor (storage, zaguan), timber upper floor (living); sliding capiz-shell windows (ventilation + light); ventanillas; overhanging eaves (tropical response); the *volada* (cantilevered gallery).
  - **Churches:** fortress-like (earthquake-resistant "Earthquake Baroque"); thick stone walls, buttresses; retablo altars; three-aisle basilican plan; detached campanile (belfry) as seismic strategy.
  - **Urbanism:** plaza complex (church + plaza + municipio + mercado) per Laws of the Indies; Intramuros (walled city, 1593+).
- **Key examples:** San Agustin Church Manila (1571/1607, UNESCO WHS); Paoay Church Ilocos (baroque-buttressed); Santa Maria Church; Miag-ao Church (fortress-facade); Vigan heritage town (UNESCO).
- **Exam angle:** bahay na bato anatomy (zaguan, volada, ventanilla, capiz); Earthquake Baroque features; plaza complex.

### 1.4 American Colonial & Commonwealth (1898–1946)
- **Context:** new public institutions; City Beautiful movement; neoclassicism as state architecture.
- **Characteristics:** neoclassical civic buildings; steel-reinforced concrete introduction; the "burnham plan" for Manila (Daniel Burnham, 1905) and Baguio; art deco cinemas/theaters in the 1930s.
- **Key works:** Manila Post Office (Ralph Harrington Doane/Tomás Mapúa-era associates); Legislative Building (now National Museum); Manila City Hall; Jones Bridge; art-deco Capitol Theater (Juan Nakpil).
- **Filipino architects emerge:** Tomás Mapúa (founder of Mapúa Institute of Technology, 1925), Juan Arellano, Juan Nakpil.

### 1.5 Modern & Contemporary (1946–present)
- **Context:** postwar rebuilding; international modernism meets tropical response; National Artist recognition (1970s–).
- **Characteristics:** reinforced concrete, brise-soleil/shading, pilotis, floating volumes; Locsin's "floating volume" over open ground plane; Mañosa's neo-vernacular (bahay kubo re-interpreted in modern materials).
- **Key works:** CCP Complex (Locsin); PICC; NAIA 1; FEU campus (Pablo Antonio); Santo Domingo Church (Zaragoza); Coconut Palace (Mañosa).
- **Exam angle:** match architect → work → style; tropical modernism traits.

## 4. Terminology (Layer 11 — PH glossary)

- **Bahay kubo** — nipa hut; indigenous stilt house
- **Bahay na bato** — Spanish-colonial "stone house" (stone lower, timber upper)
- **Zaguan** — ground-floor carriage/storage space of bahay na bato
- **Ventanilla** — small window below the main sliding window (ventilation)
- **Volada** — cantilevered upper-floor gallery
- **Capiz** — translucent shell used in sliding windows
- **Okir / ukkil** — Maranao/Muslim geometric-floral carving
- **Torogan** — Maranao royal house; **Panolong** — winglike end beams
- **Retablo** — altarpiece; **Campanile** — detached bell tower
- **Earthquake Baroque** — fortress-church style with heavy buttresses (Ilocos)
- **Plaza complex** — church-square-government-market arrangement (Laws of the Indies)

## 5. Common Mistakes (Layer 12)

- Confusing **bahay kubo** (indigenous) with **bahay na bato** (colonial hybrid) — know the distinguishing anatomy.
- Attributing **San Agustin** (1571/1607, oldest stone church) to the wrong order/period.
- Matching **Locsin → Coconut Palace** (wrong — that's Mañosa); Locsin → CCP/PICC/NAIA1.
- Forgetting **Ildefonso Santos** is LANDSCAPE (father of PH landscape architecture) — he is a National Artist for Architecture but his medium is landscape.
- Confusing Nakpil's neoclassic (Quezon Hall) with Antonio's modernism (FEU) — style-to-architect matching is a classic MCQ.
