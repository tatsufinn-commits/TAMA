## 2. CANONICAL THEORISTS & MOVEMENTS MATRIX

| Theorist & Epoch | Landmark Text / Manifesto | Core Architectural Philosophy | Iconic Built Manifestation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Louis Sullivan** (1896) | *"The Tall Office Building Artistically Considered"* | **"Form follows function"**: The architectural form of a building must express its internal biological and structural purpose (Tripartite High-Rise: Base, Shaft, Capital). | **Wainwright Building** (St. Louis), Carson Pirie Scott. |
| **Le Corbusier** (1923) | *Vers une architecture (Towards a New Architecture)* | **"The house is a machine for living in"**: Industrial standardized modular precision, the 5 Points of Modern Architecture, and the Modulor. | **Villa Savoye**, Unité d'Habitation. |
| **Ludwig Mies van der Rohe** (1947) | *Bauhaus / IIT Inaugural Lectures* | **"Less is more"** / **"God is in the details"**: Universal space, pure structural steel clarity, transparent glass minimalism. | **Seagram Building** (NYC), Farnsworth House, Barcelona Pavilion. |
| **Robert Venturi** (1966) | *Complexity and Contradiction in Architecture* | **"Less is a bore"**: Post-Modernism manifesto championing historical allusion, ambiguity, irony, and visual decoration over functionalist sterility. | **Vanna Venturi House** (Chestnut Hill, PA). |
| **Kenneth Frampton** (1983) | *"Towards a Critical Regionalism"* | **Critical Regionalism**: Resistance against universalizing corporate modernism through tectonic expression, local topography, microclimate response, and light tactile quality. | Works of Jørn Utzon (Bagsvaerd Church), Alvaro Siza, Tadao Ando. |
| **Christian Norberg-Schulz** (1979) | *Genius Loci: Towards a Phenomenology of Architecture* | **Genius Loci (Spirit of the Place)**: Phenomenological space where architecture concretizes human existential dwelling and sensory belonging. | Vernacular settlement morphologies. |
| **Colin Rowe** (1947) | *"The Mathematics of the Ideal Villa"* | Comparative formal analysis revealing mathematical grid and proportion parallels between Andrea Palladio (Villa Foscari) and Le Corbusier (Villa Stein). | Villa Stein-de Monzie / Villa Malcontenta. |

## 3. SOCRATIC EXAM SIMULATION (BOARD-STYLE SCENARIOS)

#### Question 1 (Theoretical Axioms & Authorship):
*"A gentle manifesto against the puritanically moral language of modern architecture, proclaiming that architecture must embrace ambiguity, contradiction, and hybrid elements rather than clean functional simplification."* Which seminal text and architect does this describe?
- A) *Towards a New Architecture* by Le Corbusier
- B) *Complexity and Contradiction in Architecture* by Robert Venturi
- C) *The Seven Lamps of Architecture* by John Ruskin
- D) *Delirious New York* by Rem Koolhaas
> **Correct Answer: B**  
> **Distractor Trap Analysis**:
> * Robert Venturi's 1966 book *Complexity and Contradiction in Architecture* founded the Post-Modernist movement by attacking modernist orthodoxy and coining the famous retort *"Less is a bore"*.
> * Le Corbusier (Option A) argued for purist functional geometry (*"machine for living"*).
