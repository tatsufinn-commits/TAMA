## 1. TOPOGRAPHIC SLOPE ANALYSIS & BUILDABILITY CRITERIA

* **0% to 3% Slope (Flat / Level):** Excellent for large-footprint commercial buildings, sports complexes; requires careful surface drainage grading.
* **3% to 8% Slope (Gently Rolling):** Ideal for general residential and institutional building construction with minimal cut-and-fill.
* **8% to 15% Slope (Moderately Steeper):** Suitable for stepped / terraced hillside residential clusters; roads must follow contour lines.
* **15% to 18% Slope (Steep):** Approaching maximum limit for standard vehicular access and residential development.
* **> 18% Slope (Critical Slopes):** Legally classified as **Non-Buildable Forest / Conservation Lands** per Presidential Decree No. 705 (Revised Forestry Code of the Philippines).

## 2. TROPICAL MICROCLIMATE & SOLAR ORIENTATION

* **Solar Sun-Path (Philippines $4^\circ\text{ to }21^\circ\text{ North Latitude}$):**
  * Long axis of buildings oriented **East-West**.
  * Major window glazing oriented **North and South**.
  * East and West façades minimized or protected with vertical louvers and service cores (stairs, elevators, washrooms).
* **Prevailing Wind Regimes (Philippine Atmospheric Patterns):**
  * **Northeast Monsoon (*Amihan*):** Cool, dry winds blowing from the Northeast between **November and February**.
  * **Southwest Monsoon (*Habagat*):** Warm, humid, rain-bearing winds blowing from the Southwest between **June and October**.
  * **Trade Winds (*Silangan*):** Steady easterly winds during transition months (March to May).
