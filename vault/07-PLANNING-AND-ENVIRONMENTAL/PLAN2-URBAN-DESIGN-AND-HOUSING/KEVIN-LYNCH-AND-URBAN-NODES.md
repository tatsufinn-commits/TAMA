## 1. THEORETICAL & METHODOLOGICAL FOUNDATIONS

### 1.1 Kevin Lynch's 5 Elements of Urban Form (*The Image of the City*)
Human cognitive mapping and legibility of the built environment are structured through 5 primary elements:

```
                              KEVIN LYNCH'S 5 URBAN ELEMENTS
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ 1. PATHS: Channels along which the observer moves (Streets, walkways, transit)   │
  ├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 2. EDGES: Linear elements not used as paths; lateral boundaries (Rivers, walls)  │
  ├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 3. DISTRICTS: Medium-to-large city sections with common identifying character    │
  ├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 4. NODES: Strategic focal points, junctions, or intersections of paths (Plazas)  │
  ├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 5. LANDMARKS: External point references; distinctive physical objects (Monuments)│
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 4. WORKED MATHEMATICAL DERIVATIONS & SUBDIVISION LOT YIELD

### 4.1 Master Plan Subdivision Lot Yield & Efficiency Calculation
**Problem**: An architect is preparing a BP 220 Economic Housing Subdivision Master Plan on a site area $A = 5.0\text{ hectares}$ ($50,000\text{ m}^2$) in Cavite.  
- Mandatory Non-Saleable Open Space Allocation (Roads, Parks, Community Facilities) = $30\%$ ($15,000\text{ m}^2$).  
- Net Saleable Area = $70\%$ ($35,000\text{ m}^2$).  
- Housing Mix: $60\%$ Rowhouses (minimum lot $36.0\text{ m}^2$, $4.0\text{m} \times 9.0\text{m}$) and $40\%$ Duplexes (minimum lot $54.0\text{ m}^2$, $6.0\text{m} \times 9.0\text{m}$).  
- Determine total housing lot yield and gross development density (units/hectare).

```
Step 1: Partition Net Saleable Land Area
1. Area for Rowhouses = 35,000 m² × 0.60 = 21,000 m².
2. Area for Duplexes = 35,000 m² × 0.40 = 14,000 m².

Step 2: Calculate Number of Saleable Housing Lots
1. Rowhouse Lot Yield = 21,000 m² / 36.0 m²/lot = 583.3 ➔ 583 Rowhouse Lots.
2. Duplex Lot Yield = 14,000 m² / 54.0 m²/lot = 259.2 ➔ 259 Duplex Lots.
Total Subdivision Lot Yield = 583 + 259 = 842 Residential Lots.

Step 3: Compute Gross Density
Gross Density = Total Housing Units / Total Site Area
Gross Density = 842 units / 5.0 hectares = 168.4 units per hectare.
➔ Compliance Check: Satisfies high-density economic housing benchmarks under BP 220.
```

## 6. SOCRATIC EXAM SIMULATION (BOARD-STYLE SCENARIOS)

#### Question 1 (Housing Standards - Rowhouse Block Limits):
Under BP 220 (Socialized and Economic Housing), what is the maximum number of rowhouse dwelling units permitted in a single continuous unbroken block before an alley or interconnecting pathwalk is mandatory?
- A) 10 units
- B) 15 units
- C) 20 units (or maximum $100\text{m}$ block length)
- D) 30 units
> **Correct Answer: C**  
> **Distractor Trap Analysis**:
> * Both BP 220 and PD 957 strictly cap rowhouse strings at **20 units per block** or a maximum block length of **$100.0\text{m}$** to maintain emergency fire access, prevent monotonous urban sprawl, and facilitate cross-block pedestrian circulation.
