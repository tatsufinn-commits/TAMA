## 2. TECHNICAL MECHANICS, SERVICE PHASES & METHODS OF COMPENSATION

### 2.1 SPP 202 Regular Design Services Phase Breakdown & Fee Release Schedule
Under SPP 202, the Architect's Basic Fee is billed progressively across 5 milestone phases:

| Phase Number & Name | Milestone Deliverables | Percentage of Basic Fee | Cumulative Payment |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Phase 1: Schematic Design (SD)** | Site analysis, conceptual diagrams, preliminary spatial plans ($1:200$), statement of probable construction cost. | **$15\%$** | **$15\%$** |
| **Phase 2: Design Development (DD)** | Developed drawings ($1:100$), elevations, sections, outline specifications, updated cost estimate. | **$20\%$** | **$35\%$** |
| **Phase 3: Contract Documents (CD)** | Complete signed and sealed architectural and engineering working drawings, technical specifications (CSI format), BOQ. | **$40\%$** | **$75\%$** (Upon completion of CD) |
| **Phase 4: Bidding & Negotiation** | Preparation of bid documents, evaluation of contractor proposals, assistance in awarding contract. | **$5\%$** | **$80\%$** |
| **Phase 5: Construction Administration (CA)**| Periodic site visits, issuance of certificates for payment, interpretation of drawings, final punch-listing. | **$20\%$** | **$100\%$** (Progressive release during construction) |

### 2.2 Standard Methods of Architectural Compensation:
1. **Percentage of Project Construction Cost (PPCC):** Traditional standard where professional fee is a percentage ($6\%–10\%$) of the actual completed construction cost.
2. **Multiple of Direct Personnel Expense (MDPE):** Standard for non-standard advisory services:
   $$\text{Fee} = \text{Direct Cost } (AN + CN + TN) \times M + R$$
   *(where $M = \text{Multiplier ranging from } 1.5 \text{ to } 2.5$, $R = \text{Reimbursable expenses}$)*.
3. **Professional Fee Plus Expenses:** Fixed lump sum professional fee with actual direct expenses reimbursed by the Owner.
4. **Lump Sum / Fixed Fee:** Fixed contract amount agreed upfront (used when project scope is clearly defined).
5. **Per Diem / Hourly Rate:** Billing by actual hours rendered for expert witness testimony or feasibility consultations.

## 4. WORKED MATHEMATICAL DERIVATIONS & FEE CALCULATIONS

### 4.1 Professional Fee Calculation under SPP 202 & Progress Billing
**Problem**: An Architect is hired under SPP 202 for a 5-storey Residential Condominium (Group 2 Structure) with an estimated Project Construction Cost $PCC = \text{Php 60,000,000.00}$. The agreed Basic Fee is **$7.0\%$** of PCC.  
- The Architect completes Schematic Design (SD) and Design Development (DD), receiving all milestone billings.  
- During the Contract Documents (CD) phase, the Owner abruptly terminates the project due to financial insolvency before bidding.  
- Calculate: (1) Total Basic Fee, (2) Total earned fee payable to the Architect upon completion of the CD phase, and (3) Net remaining balance due from the Owner.

```
Step 1: Calculate Total Basic Professional Fee
Total Basic Fee = PCC × Agreed Percentage = Php 60,000,000.00 × 0.07 = Php 4,200,000.00.

Step 2: Determine Milestone Earned Percentages under SPP 202
1. Schematic Design Phase (15%) = 0.15 × Php 4,200,000.00 = Php 630,000.00 (Paid).
2. Design Development Phase (20%) = 0.20 × Php 4,200,000.00 = Php 840,000.00 (Paid).
   Total Paid to Date (35%) = Php 630,000.00 + Php 840,000.00 = Php 1,470,000.00.

3. Contract Documents Phase (40%) = 0.40 × Php 4,200,000.00 = Php 1,680,000.00.
Cumulative Earned Percentage through CD Phase = 15% + 20% + 40% = 75% of Basic Fee.
Total Earned Value through CD Phase = 0.75 × Php 4,200,000.00 = Php 3,150,000.00.

Step 3: Calculate Remaining Balance Due upon Project Cancellation
Remaining Balance = Total Earned (75%) - Cumulative Payments Received (35%)
Remaining Balance = Php 3,150,000.00 - Php 1,470,000.00 = Php 1,680,000.00.

➔ Legal Outcome: Under SPP 202 Section 6, the Owner must immediately remit Php 1,680,000.00 
   to the Architect for completing the Contract Documents phase, even if the project is never built!
```

## 6. SOCRATIC EXAM SIMULATION (BOARD-STYLE SCENARIOS)

#### Question 1 (SPP 202 Milestone Fee Release):
Under SPP Document 202, once the Architect has completed and submitted all signed and sealed architectural and engineering working drawings, technical specifications, and general conditions (Contract Documents Phase), what cumulative percentage of the Basic Professional Fee is legally earned by the Architect?
- A) $35\%$
- B) $50\%$
- C) $75\%$
- D) $85\%$
> **Correct Answer: C**  
> **Distractor Trap Analysis**:
> * Cumulative milestone releases under SPP 202 are: Schematic Design ($15\%$) + Design Development ($20\%$) + Contract Documents ($40\%$) = **$75\%$ Total Cumulative Fee**. The remaining $25\%$ covers Bidding ($5\%$) and Periodic Construction Administration ($20\%$).
