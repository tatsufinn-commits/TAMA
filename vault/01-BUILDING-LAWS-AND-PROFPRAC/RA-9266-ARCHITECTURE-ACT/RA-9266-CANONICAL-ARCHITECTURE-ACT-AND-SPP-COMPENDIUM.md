## 2. EXECUTIVE FOUNDATION: THE SOLE JURISDICTION & ETHICAL CALLING OF THE ARCHITECT

Republic Act No. 9266 establishes the statutory doctrine that **architecture is an exclusive professional practice**. The preparation, signing, and sealing of architectural plans, spatial designs, interior architecture, and site development documents are the sole legal purview of Registered and Licensed Architects (RLAs) [L][A].

### The Architect's Code of Ethical Conduct & Professional Creed
Under PRBoA Resolution No. 2, Series of 2006, an architect's primary ethical commitment is divided into five cardinal relationships:
1. **To the Public:** Advocate for life safety, cultural heritage, environmental conservation, and honest public representations.
2. **To the Client:** Guard client interests with fiduciary integrity, provide honest cost estimates, and charge fair, transparent fees.
3. **To the Contractor:** Act as an impartial arbiter in interpreting contract documents without favoritism or secret kickbacks.
4. **To Manufacturers & Suppliers:** Never accept discounts, rebates, or commissions that compromise design objectivity.
5. **To Colleagues & the Profession:** Never undercut fees, compete unfairly, criticize a colleague's work publicly, or review another architect's project without prior written notification.

## 3. MASTER GLOSSARY & ACRONYMS

* **RLA:** Registered and Licensed Architect — a person holding a valid Certificate of Registration and Professional Identification Card issued by the PRC and PRBoA [L].
* **PRBoA:** Professional Regulatory Board of Architecture — the government collegial body that regulates architectural education, examinations, and professional practice in the Philippines [L].
* **IAPOA:** Integrated and Accredited Professional Organization of Architects — the national official organization of architects accredited by PRC (United Architects of the Philippines / UAP) [L].
* **Instruments of Service:** Architectural drawings, specifications, calculations, scale models, and contract documents prepared by an architect, which remain the **intellectual property of the architect** under §33 of RA 9266 [L].
* **Dry-Sealing:** The illegal and criminal act of signing and sealing architectural documents that were not prepared by or under the direct personal supervision of the signing architect (§32) [L].
* **Structural Conceptualization:** The architectural design of the overall building structural framing system, grid layout, column placements, and shear wall geometry (§3) [L].
* **SPP:** Standards of Professional Practice — PRBoA-promulgated documents (SPP 200 Series) defining the scope of architectural services, deliverables, and compensation methods [L].
* **MDPE (Multiple of Direct Personnel Expense):** A standard method of compensation where the direct personnel cost is multiplied by a factor ($1.5\text{ to }2.5$) to cover overhead and professional profit [L].
* **PPCC (Percentage of Project Construction Cost):** A compensation method where the architect's fee is computed as a percentage of the total completed construction cost [L].
* **CIAC:** Construction Industry Arbitration Commission — the quasi-judicial body created under Executive Order No. 1008 with original and exclusive jurisdiction over construction contract disputes in the Philippines [L].

## 4. GOVERNING LAWS, STATUTORY SECTIONS & SPP 200 SERIES MATRIX [L]

### A. Core Statutory Provisions of RA 9266 (The Architecture Act of 2004)

| Section | Title & Focus | Exact Statutory Rule & Exam Discipline |
|---|---|---|
| **Section 3** | **Definitions & Scope** | Defines Architecture, Architect, General Practice, Scope of Practice, Structural Conceptualization, and Architectural Interiors [L]. |
| **Section 13** | **Qualifications for ALE** | Filipino citizen (or qualified foreign national under reciprocity), good moral character, BS Architecture graduate, **$2\text{ years}$ ($3,840\text{ hours}$)** diverse architectural experience logbook under a mentor RLA [L]. |
| **Section 14** | **Subjects for Licensure** | 3 Groups: (1) History/Theory/Planning & Practice (30%); (2) Structural Design, Building Materials, Methods & Utilities (30%); (3) Architectural Design & Site Planning (40%) [L]. |
| **Section 15** | **Passing Rating** | Weighted general average of **$\ge 70\%$**, with **no grade lower than $50\%$** in any individual subject [L]. |
| **Section 20** | **Seal and Use of Seal** | Prescribes the official RLA circular seal ($45\text{ mm}$ diameter). Mandatory on all architectural plans, specs, and drawings. Unlicensed persons using the seal are criminally liable [L]. |
| **Section 25** | **Registration Required** | No person shall practice architecture, prepare architectural plans, or use the title "Architect" without a valid PRC license [L]. |
| **Section 32** | **Signing & Sealing Prohibition** | Unlawful for any architect to sign/seal documents made under another architect's supervision (**Dry-sealing prohibition**). Revocation of license and criminal penalties apply [L]. |
| **Section 33** | **Ownership of Plans** | Signed and sealed documents are **instruments of service** and remain the **intellectual property of the architect**, whether the project is executed or not. Client cannot reuse plans without written contract [L]. |
| **Section 34** | **Corporate Practice** | Architectural partnerships/corporations must be registered with SEC and PRBoA, with **at least $75\%$ of voting stock and ownership held by RLAs** [L]. |
| **Section 38** | **Foreign Reciprocity & TSP** | Foreign architects must secure a **Temporary/Special Permit (TSP)**, work in joint venture / association with a Filipino RLA, and prove reciprocity laws in their home country [L]. |
| **Section 44** | **Penalties & Fines** | Fine of **₱100,000 to ₱5,000,000**, or imprisonment of **6 months to 6 years** (or both) for unauthorized practice or illegal sealing [L]. |

### C. SPP 202 Regular Design Services Phase Breakdown & Fee Milestone Schedule

Under SPP 202, the Basic Professional Fee (BPF) is released progressively across 5 standard milestone phases:

```
  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │               SPP 202 PROGRESSIVE FEE RELEASE MILESTONES                  │
  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  
   PHASE 1: SCHEMATIC DESIGN (SD) ──────────────► 15% Cumulative
   • Preliminary sketches, space allocations,
     statement of probable construction cost.
  
   PHASE 2: DESIGN DEVELOPMENT (DD) ────────────► 35% Cumulative (15% + 20%)
   • Detailed floor plans, elevations, sections,
     outline specifications, updated cost estimate.
  
   PHASE 3: CONTRACT DOCUMENTS (CD) ────────────► 75% Cumulative (35% + 40%)
   • Complete signed & sealed working drawings,
     technical specs, bill of quantities (BOQ).
  
   PHASE 4: BIDDING & NEGOTIATION ──────────────► 80% Cumulative (75% + 5%)
   • Bid tender preparation, contractor evaluation,
     award recommendation.
  
   PHASE 5: CONSTRUCTION ADMINISTRATION (CA) ──► 100% Cumulative (80% + 20%)
   • Periodic site inspections, issuance of payment
     certificates, punch-listing, Certificate of Final Inspection.
```

## 5. FORMULAS & WORKED CALCULATION EXAMPLES [L]

### Canonical Equations

1. **Multiple of Direct Personnel Expense (MDPE):**
   $$\text{Fee} = (\text{Direct Personnel Expense} \times M) + R$$
   $$\text{Direct Personnel Expense (DPE)} = \sum (\text{Hours Rendered} \times \text{Hourly Base Salary})$$
   *(where Multiplier $M = 1.5\text{ to }2.5$, covers office overhead + profit; $R = \text{Direct reimbursables}$).*

2. **Basic Professional Fee (BPF):**
   $$\text{BPF} = \text{Project Construction Cost (PCC)} \times \text{Applicable Percentage } (\%)$$

3. **Design-Build by Administration Fee:**
   $$\text{Total Fee} = \text{BPF (Design)} + 7\% \text{ Construction Management Fee}$$

4. **Design-Build on Guaranteed Maximum Cost (GMC):**
   $$\text{Total Fee} = \text{BPF (Design)} + 10\% \text{ Design-Build Fee}$$

### 🏛️ WORKED CALCULATION 1: 6-Storey Commercial Office Building ($₱120,000,000$ PCC)
**Scenario:** A client commissions an architect for complete SPP 202 Regular Design Services for a Group 3 Commercial Office Building with a Project Construction Cost of **$₱120,000,000$**. The agreed Basic Professional Fee (BPF) rate is **$6.0\%$**.

**Step 1: Calculate Total Basic Professional Fee (BPF)**
$$\text{Total BPF} = ₱120,000,000 \times 0.06 = \mathbf{₱7,200,000.00}$$

**Step 2: Calculate Fee Release per Milestone Phase**
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Phase 1: Schematic Design } (15\%) &= ₱7,200,000 \times 0.15 = \mathbf{₱1,080,000.00} \quad (\text{Cum: } ₱1,080,000) \\
\text{Phase 2: Design Development } (20\%) &= ₱7,200,000 \times 0.20 = \mathbf{₱1,440,000.00} \quad (\text{Cum: } ₱2,520,000) \\
\text{Phase 3: Contract Documents } (40\%) &= ₱7,200,000 \times 0.40 = \mathbf{₱2,880,000.00} \quad (\text{Cum: } ₱5,400,000 = \mathbf{75\%}) \\
\text{Phase 4: Bidding & Negotiation } (5\%) &= ₱7,200,000 \times 0.05 = \mathbf{₱360,000.00} \quad (\text{Cum: } ₱5,760,000 = \mathbf{80\%}) \\
\text{Phase 5: Construction Admin } (20\%) &= ₱7,200,000 \times 0.20 = \mathbf{₱1,440,000.00} \quad (\text{Cum: } ₱7,200,000 = \mathbf{100\%})
\end{aligned}$$

*Exam Note:* If the client decides to cancel the project after receiving complete signed and sealed working drawings (Phase 3), the architect is legally entitled to **$75\%$ of the total BPF** ($₱5,400,000.00$) [L]!

### 🏛️ WORKED CALCULATION 2: Site Feasibility Study (MDPE Method)
**Scenario:** An architectural firm renders SPP 201 Pre-Design feasibility services with the following recorded hours:
* Principal Architect: $30\text{ hours}$ @ $₱2,000.00/\text{hr} = ₱60,000.00$
* Senior Urban Planner: $80\text{ hours}$ @ $₱1,200.00/\text{hr} = ₱96,000.00$
* Research Assistant: $150\text{ hours}$ @ $₱500.00/\text{hr} = ₱75,000.00$
* Multiplier $M = 2.2$
* Reimbursable Expenses $R$ (soil test surveys, printing, aerial drones): $₱48,000.00$

**Step 1: Calculate Direct Personnel Expense (DPE)**
$$\text{DPE} = ₱60,000 + ₱96,000 + ₱75,000 = \mathbf{₱231,000.00}$$

**Step 2: Apply Multiplier and Add Reimbursables**
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Professional Fee} &= (\text{DPE} \times M) + R \\
&= (₱231,000.00 \times 2.2) + ₱48,000.00 \\
&= ₱508,200.00 + ₱48,000.00 = \mathbf{₱556,200.00}
\end{aligned}$$

## 6. CONTRACTUAL INSTRUMENTS, UAP DOC 301 & CIAC ARBITRATION [I][L]

### A. UAP Document 301: General Conditions of a Construction Contract
* **Contract Documents Hierarchy:** Agreement $\rightarrow$ Special Provisions $\rightarrow$ General Conditions $\rightarrow$ Specifications $\rightarrow$ Drawings.
* **Shop Drawings:** Drawings prepared by the Contractor/fabricator showing exact installation details; reviewed and approved by the AoR before fabrication.
* **Retention Guarantee (Holdback):** $10\%$ retained from each progress billing until final completion and acceptance.
* **Defects Liability / Guarantee Period:** Contractor warrants the structure against defects in materials and workmanship for **$1\text{ year}$ (12 months)** from the date of Certificate of Final Acceptance.

---

### B. CIAC Arbitration (Executive Order No. 1008)
* **Exclusive Jurisdiction:** The **Construction Industry Arbitration Commission (CIAC)** has original, exclusive jurisdiction over all construction disputes in the Philippines where the contract contains an arbitration clause.
* **Finality of Award:** CIAC arbitral awards are legally binding, executory, and appealable only on pure questions of law to the Court of Appeals under Rule 43. Factual findings of CIAC arbitrators are final and unappealable.

## 7. EXAM TRAPS & COMMONLY CONFUSED CODE PROVISIONS

```text
 ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │                  EXAM TRAPS: RA 9266 & PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE               │
 └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **TRAP 1: Ownership of Plans vs Physical Possession (§33):**
  * The Client owns the *physical paper blueprints* they paid for.
  * The Architect retains the **intellectual property, copyright, and ownership of the design**.
  * *Trap:* The client CANNOT use the same plans to construct a duplicate building on another site without a separate contract and fee!
* **TRAP 2: Cumulative Fee at Contract Documents Completion ($75\%$ vs $80\%$):**
  * Upon delivery of complete signed and sealed working drawings (Phase 3), the cumulative fee is **$75\%$** ($15\% + 20\% + 40\%$).
  * Bidding ($5\%$) brings it to $80\%$. Construction Admin ($20\%$) completes it to $100\%$.
* **TRAP 3: Civil Liability under Article 1723 of the Civil Code:**
  * The Architect-of-Record and Engineer are held civilly liable for damages if the building collapses within **$15\text{ years}$** from completion due to defect in plans or specifications.
  * Prescriptive period to file suit: **$10\text{ years}$** from the date of collapse.
* **TRAP 4: Dry-Sealing Criminal Penalties (§32 & §44):**
  * Affixing a seal to plans prepared by an unlicensed draftsman or another architect without direct supervision is a **criminal offense** punishable by fine ($₱100\text{k}\text{--}₱5\text{M}$) and prison ($6\text{ months}\text{--}6\text{ years}$).
* **TRAP 5: Corporate Practice RLA Ownership Share (§34):**
  * At least **$75\%$** of ownership and voting stock in architectural corporations/partnerships must be held by Registered and Licensed Architects.

## 8. HISTORICAL EVOLUTION OF PHILIPPINE ARCHITECTURAL LAWS

```text
 ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │                  EVOLUTION OF PHILIPPINE ARCHITECTURE LAWS                 │
 └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Act No. 2985 (February 23, 1921):** First legislation regulating the practice of "Engineers and Architects" under the American Insular Government.
* **Republic Act No. 545 (June 17, 1950):** The first independent Architecture Law separating architects from civil engineers, creating the Board of Examiners for Architects.
* **Republic Act No. 9266 (March 17, 2004):** Repealed RA 545; modernized the definition of architecture, created PRBoA under PRC, established exclusive architectural signing rights, mandated CPD, and enacted severe criminal penalties for unauthorized practice.
