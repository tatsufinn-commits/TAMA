# PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE 1 — CONTRACT DOCUMENTS & BIDDING PROCEDURES
**Authority:** UAP Document 301 (General Conditions), RA 9266 & SPP Document 202  
**High-Yield Score:** 9.0 / 10 (Critical for Mapúa ProfPrac Departmentals & ALE Board)  

---

## 1. THE 3 KEY PROJECT PARTICIPANTS & RELATIONSHIPS

* **The Owner:** The person or entity ordering the project; pays for services; holds title to the site.
* **The Architect:** The professional registered and licensed architect (RLA); prepares instruments of service (plans, specs, estimates); acts as the Owner's technical advisor and agent during construction.
* **The Contractor:** The person or firm licensed by PCAB to execute construction work per contract documents.

---

## 2. CONTRACT DOCUMENTS COMPOSITION (UAP DOC 301)

A complete construction contract package consists of:
1. **The Agreement (Contract):** The legally binding contract document between Owner and Contractor.
2. **General Conditions (UAP Doc 301):** Standard contractual rules defining relationships, rights, duties, and liabilities.
3. **Special Provisions / Conditions:** Project-specific amendments to general conditions.
4. **Architectural & Engineering Drawings:** Architectural, Structural, Plumbing/Sanitary, Electrical, Mechanical, Fire Protection drawings signed and sealed by licensed professionals.
5. **Technical Specifications:** Written descriptions of materials, workmanship, and standards (CSI MasterFormat).
6. **Bill of Quantities (BOQ) / Cost Estimate:** Itemized material and labor breakdown.

## 3. BIDDING PROCEDURES & CONTRACTOR BONDS

| Bond / Security Type | Standard Percentage | Purpose |
|---|:---:|---|
| **Bidder's Bond (Proposal Guarantee)** | **$2\% \text{ to } 5\%$** | Guarantees the winning bidder will enter into contract and provide performance bond. |
| **Performance Bond** | **$15\% \text{ (Surety)} / 30\% \text{ (Letter of Credit)}$** | Guarantees the contractor will complete work per plans and specifications. |
| **Payment Bond (Labor & Materials)** | **$15\% \text{ to } 30\%$** | Guarantees the contractor will pay all subcontractors, laborers, and material suppliers. |
| **Guarantee / Maintenance Bond** | **$10\% \text{ to } 20\%$** | Covers latent defects in construction for a period of **1 year** after final acceptance. |
| **Retention Fee** | **$10\%$** | Deducted from every progress billing; released upon final turnover and acceptance. |
