## 2. EXECUTIVE FOUNDATION: UNIVERSAL DESIGN & ANTHROPOMETRIC CLEARANCES

Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 establishes the fundamental civil right of Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) to independent mobility, dignity, and full integration into the built environment. In architectural space planning, accessibility is governed by the physical dimensions of standard manual and motorized wheelchairs and anthropometric reach envelopes [L][A].

   • Comfortable Side Reach: 0.70 m to 1.05 m above floor

## 3. MASTER GLOSSARY & ACRONYMS

* **PWD:** Person with Disability — an individual suffering from any restriction or lack of ability to perform an activity in the manner considered normal for a human being [L].
* **NCDA:** National Council on Disability Affairs — the government agency tasked with formulating policies and coordinating PWD welfare and accessibility standards [L].
* **Accessible Route:** A continuous, unobstructed path connecting all accessible elements and spaces in a building, accessible parking, passenger loading zones, and public streets [L].
* **Curb Ramp:** A short sloped ramp cutting through a sidewalk curb to allow smooth wheelchair transition between the sidewalk surface and the street gutter [L].
* **Dropped Curb:** A lowered sidewalk section at pedestrian crossings where the curb is flush or has a maximum lip of $25\text{ mm}$ [L].
* **Tactile Surface:** A textured paving surface (e.g. truncated blister domes or directional grooves) detectable by cane or foot, alerting visually impaired pedestrians of curbs, hazards, or changes in direction [L].
* **ISA (International Symbol of Access):** The internationally recognized graphic symbol showing a person in a wheelchair in white on a blue background (Pantone 294C), required on all accessible entrances, facilities, parking slots, and elevators [L].
* **Lever-Type Hardware:** Door handles and faucets operable with a closed fist or elbow requiring not more than $22\text{ N}$ ($5\text{ lbf}$) operating force without twisting of the wrist [L].
* **Clear Width:** The unobstructed net opening dimension of a doorway, corridor, or ramp, measured between the face of the door open at $90^\circ$ and the door stop, or between interior handrails/walls [L].

## 5. FORMULAS & WORKED CALCULATION EXAMPLES [L]

### Canonical Accessibility Equations

1. **Ramp Slope & Run Formula:**
   $$\text{Slope } S = \frac{\text{Rise } (H)}{\text{Run } (L)} \le \frac{1}{12} \implies L_{\text{min}} = H \times 12$$
2. **Intermediate Landings Count (every $6.00\text{ m}$ run):**
   $$N_{\text{intermediate}} = \left\lfloor \frac{L_{\text{sloped}} - 0.01}{6.00\text{ m}} \right\rfloor$$
3. **Total Linear Footprint of Straight Ramp System:**
   $$L_{\text{footprint}} = L_{\text{sloped}} + (N_{\text{intermediate}} \times 1.50\text{ m}) + L_{\text{top\_landing}} (1.50\text{ m}) + L_{\text{bottom\_landing}} (1.50\text{ m})$$
4. **Accessible Parking Slot Ratio Allocation:**
   $$\text{Accessible Slots} = \begin{cases} 
   1 & \text{for } 1 \le N \le 25 \\
   2 & \text{for } 26 \le N \le 50 \\
   3 & \text{for } 51 \le N \le 75 \\
   4 & \text{for } 76 \le N \le 100 \\
   4 + \left\lceil \frac{N - 100}{50} \right\rceil & \text{for } N > 100 
   \end{cases}$$

### 🏛️ WORKED CALCULATION 1: University Academic Hall Entrance Ramp
**Scenario:** A new Mapúa Architecture studio building has a finished ground floor level elevated **$1.20\text{ m}$** above the sidewalk grade. Design a fully compliant BP 344 accessible ramp system.

**Step 1: Calculate Minimum Sloped Ramp Run**
* Using maximum statutory slope $1:12$:
$$L_{\text{sloped}} = H \times 12 = 1.20\text{ m} \times 12 = \mathbf{14.40\text{ m}}$$

**Step 2: Determine Required Intermediate Landings**
* Maximum single run between landings is $6.00\text{ m}$.
$$N_{\text{intermediate}} = \left\lfloor \frac{14.40 - 0.01}{6.00} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor 2.398 \right\rfloor = \mathbf{2 \text{ intermediate landings}}$$
* Divide into **3 equal runs**:
$$L_{\text{run}} = \frac{14.40\text{ m}}{3} = \mathbf{4.80\text{ m per run}} \quad (\text{Since } 4.80\text{ m} \le 6.00\text{ m}, \text{COMPLIANT})$$
* Rise per run $= \frac{1.20\text{ m}}{3} = 0.40\text{ m} = 400\text{ mm}$.

**Step 3: Calculate Total Straight Linear Footprint**
* Sloped runs: $3 \times 4.80\text{ m} = 14.40\text{ m}$
* 2 Intermediate Landings ($1.50\text{ m}$ each): $2 \times 1.50\text{ m} = 3.00\text{ m}$
* Top Landing ($1.50\text{ m}$) + Bottom Landing ($1.50\text{ m}$): $3.00\text{ m}$
$$\mathbf{L_{\text{total\_footprint}}} = 14.40\text{ m} + 3.00\text{ m} + 3.00\text{ m} = \mathbf{20.40\text{ m}}$$

**Step 4: Check Handrails and Curbs**
* Clear width between handrails: $\ge 1.20\text{ m}$.
* Upper handrail at $0.90\text{ m}$, lower handrail at $0.70\text{ m}$.
* Extensions: $0.30\text{ m}$ past top and bottom landings.
* Side protection: $0.10\text{ m}$ ($100\text{ mm}$) concrete curb along both open edges.

---

### 🏛️ WORKED CALCULATION 2: Commercial Shopping Center Parking Allocation
**Scenario:** A proposed commercial mall provides a total of **$320$ standard parking slots**. Determine the statutory number of BP 344 accessible parking slots and total dedicated parking width.

**Step 1: Calculate Number of Accessible Parking Slots**
* For the first $100$ slots: **$4\text{ accessible slots}$**
* For remaining slots above $100$: $320 - 100 = 220\text{ slots}$
$$\text{Additional Slots} = \left\lceil \frac{220}{50} \right\rceil = \left\lceil 4.4 \right\rceil = \mathbf{5 \text{ slots}}$$
$$\mathbf{\text{Total Accessible Parking Slots}} = 4 + 5 = \mathbf{9 \text{ accessible parking slots}}$$

**Step 2: Calculate Dimensions & Space Allocation**
* Width per slot $= 3.70\text{ m}$ ($2.50\text{ m}$ bay $+ 1.20\text{ m}$ shared/dedicated aisle).
* Total linear bay width for 9 dedicated slots $= 9 \times 3.70\text{ m} = \mathbf{33.30\text{ m}}$ (or paired with shared aisles at $6.20\text{ m}$ per pair).
* Maximum distance from main entrance: **$\le 30.00\text{ m}$**.

## 6. MATERIALITY, HARDWARE & TACTILE ASSEMBLIES [I][L]

1. **Non-Skid Floor Finishes:**
   * Exterior ramps, walkways, and toilet stall floors must have non-slip abrasive surfaces with a dynamic coefficient of friction $\ge 0.60$ under wet conditions.
2. **Tactile Warning Pavers (Truncated Blister Domes):**
   * Installed across the full width of walkways $300\text{ mm}$ prior to dropped curb transitions, pedestrian crossings, and top of stairwells. Domes measure $5\text{ mm}$ height with $50\text{ mm}$ center spacing.
3. **Grab Bar Anchoring & Structural Integrity:**
   * Stainless steel (AISI 304) grab bars must withstand a static point load of at least **$1.33\text{ kN}$ ($300\text{ lbf}$)** without permanent deformation. Fastened with heavy-duty toggle bolts into solid masonry or reinforced timber studs.
4. **Signage & Visual Accessibility:**
   * High-contrast characters (white on blue Pantone 294C or dark on light) with character height $\ge 25\text{ mm}$ per meter of viewing distance. Raised tactile letters ($0.8\text{ mm}$ relief) accompanied by Grade 2 Braille.

## 7. EXAM TRAPS & COMMONLY CONFUSED CODE PROVISIONS

```text
 ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │                  EXAM TRAPS: BP 344 vs NBCP vs FIRE CODE                   │
 └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **TRAP 1: Ramp Slope ($1:12$ vs $1:8$ vs $1:10$):**
  * Standard BP 344 ramp maximum slope is **$1:12$ ($8.33\%$)**.
  * $1:8$ is permitted **ONLY** for short threshold drops with vertical rise $< 50\text{ mm}$ ($2\text{ in}$).
  * *Trap:* Students often apply $1:8$ or $1:10$ to long ramps under exam trick questions!
* **TRAP 2: Door Clear Width Across 3 Philippine Laws:**
  * **BP 344 Accessibility:** Min **$0.80\text{ m}$ ($800\text{ mm}$)** clear.
  * **PD 1096 NBCP:** Min **$0.71\text{ m}$ ($710\text{ mm}$)** clear.
  * **RA 9514 Fire Code:** Min **$0.71\text{ m}$ clear, $0.90\text{ m}$ standard**.
  * *Rule:* For barrier-free accessible routes, **$0.80\text{ m}$ minimum clear width is mandatory**!
* **TRAP 3: Handrail Heights (Single vs Dual):**
  * BP 344 mandates **DUAL handrails** at **$0.70\text{ m}$ and $0.90\text{ m}$** with **$0.30\text{ m}$ extensions**.
  * General stairs under NBCP use a single rail at $0.80\text{--}0.90\text{ m}$.
* **TRAP 4: Toilet Stall Dimensions ($1.70\text{m} \times 1.80\text{m}$):**
  * Standard accessible cubicle requires **$1.70\text{ m}$ width $\times 1.80\text{ m}$ depth** (outward-swinging door).
  * Do NOT confuse with standard commercial cubicles ($0.90\text{m} \times 1.50\text{m}$).
* **TRAP 5: Parking Stall Width ($3.70\text{m}$ vs $2.50\text{m}$):**
  * Standard parking bay is $2.50\text{ m} \times 5.00\text{ m}$.
  * Accessible parking bay is **$3.70\text{ m} \times 5.00\text{ m}$** (includes $1.20\text{ m}$ access aisle).

## 8. HISTORICAL CONTEXT & LEGAL ENFORCEMENT

* **Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 (1983):** Enacted during the Batasang Pambansa era to mandate barrier-free architecture across the Philippines.
* **RA 7277 (Magna Carta for Disabled Persons, 1992):** Expanded civil rights, anti-discrimination protections, and mandatory public facility accessibility.
* **RA 10754 (2016):** Granted 20% discounts, VAT exemptions, and mandated enhanced barrier-free physical facilities in public and private commercial establishments.
* **Criminal Penalties (§4 of BP 344):** Violators face imprisonment of 1 month to 1 year, or fines from ₱2,000 to ₱5,000 (or both). In corporate violations, the President, General Manager, or Architect/Engineer of Record is directly criminally liable [L].

#### Q1. Under BP 344, what is the maximum allowable gradient/slope for pedestrian accessible ramps?
- [ ] A. $1:8$ ($12.50\%$)
- [ ] B. $1:10$ ($10.00\%$)
- [x] C. **$1:12$ ($8.33\%$)**
- [ ] D. $1:16$ ($6.25\%$)
> **Explanation:** The statutory maximum slope for general accessible ramps under BP 344 Rule II Section 2 is **$1:12$ ($8.33\%$)**. Slopes of $1:8$ are permitted only for very short vertical drops $<50\text{ mm}$ [L].

---

#### Q2. What is the maximum horizontal run permitted for an accessible ramp section before an intermediate landing must be provided?
- [ ] A. $3.00\text{ m}$
- [ ] B. $4.50\text{ m}$
- [x] C. **$6.00\text{ m}$ ($6,000\text{ mm}$)**
- [ ] D. $9.00\text{ m}$
> **Explanation:** Ramps with a $1:12$ slope must have a level landing of at least $1.50\text{ m}$ length provided for every **$6.00\text{ m}$ of horizontal run** [L].

---

#### Q3. What are the mandatory mounting heights for dual handrails along accessible ramps and stairs under BP 344?
- [ ] A. $0.60\text{ m}$ and $0.80\text{ m}$
- [x] B. **$0.70\text{ m}$ and $0.90\text{ m}$**
- [ ] C. $0.75\text{ m}$ and $1.00\text{ m}$
- [ ] D. $0.80\text{ m}$ and $1.10\text{ m}$
