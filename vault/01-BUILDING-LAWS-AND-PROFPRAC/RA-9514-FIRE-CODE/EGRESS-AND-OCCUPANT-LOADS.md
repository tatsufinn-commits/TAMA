# RA 9514 — REVISED FIRE CODE OF THE PHILIPPINES: EGRESS & OCCUPANT LOADS
**Authority:** Republic Act No. 9514 (Fire Code of the Philippines of 2008 & 2019 IRR)  
**High-Yield Score:** 9.6 / 10 (Critical for Mapúa Departmentals, Exit Exams & PRC ALE)  

---

## 1. OCCUPANT LOAD FACTORS (SECTION 10.2.5.2)

The occupant load is determined by dividing floor area by the designated factor:

| Occupancy / Use Classification | Factor (sqm per person) | Notes |
|---|:---:|---|
| **Classrooms** | **1.80** | Net area per student |
| **Shops, Laboratories, Vocational Rooms** | **4.60** | Net vocational area |
| **Assembly, Concentrated (Chairs/Auditorium)**| **0.65** | Net waiting/seating area |
| **Assembly, Unconcentrated (Tables & Chairs)**| **1.40** | Dining, banquet, restaurants |
| **Assembly, Standing Room Only** | **0.28** | Dense standing space |
| **Offices (General / Administrative)** | **9.30** | Gross office area |
| **Mercantile (Street Floor / Sales Basement)**| **2.80** | Gross retail sales area |
| **Mercantile (Upper Sales Floors)** | **5.60** | Gross upper retail floor |
| **Residential (Dwellings / Condos)** | **18.60** | Gross habitable area |
| **Storage / Warehouse** | **28.00** | Gross storage area |

---

## 2. MEANS OF EGRESS CAPACITY & DIMENSIONS (SECTION 10.2.5.4)

* **Minimum Stairway Width:**
  * $<50$ occupants: **$0.91\text{m}$ ($915\text{mm}$)**
  * $\ge 50$ occupants: **$1.12\text{m}$ ($1,120\text{mm}$)**
* **Maximum Riser Height:** **$0.20\text{m}$ ($200\text{mm}$)** (Class A: $190\text{mm}$)
* **Minimum Tread Depth:** **$0.25\text{m}$ ($250\text{mm}$)**
* **Minimum Headroom Clearance:** **$2.00\text{m}$ ($2,000\text{mm}$)**

---

## 3. MAXIMUM TRAVEL DISTANCES TO EXITS

| Building Condition | Maximum Travel Distance |
|---|:---:|
| **Without Automatic Fire Sprinkler System** | **$46.00\text{m}$ ($150\text{ft}$)** |
| **With Approved Automatic Fire Sprinkler System**| **$61.00\text{m}$ ($200\text{ft}$)** |
| **Dead-End Corridor Limit (General)** | **$6.00\text{m}$ ($20\text{ft}$)** |
| **Dead-End Corridor with Sprinklers** | **$15.00\text{m}$ ($50\text{ft}$)** |

---

## 4. NUMBER OF REQUIRED EXITS

* Occupant Load $\le 500$: **Minimum 2 Exits**
* Occupant Load $501 - 1,000$: **Minimum 3 Exits**
* Occupant Load $> 1,000$: **Minimum 4 Exits**
