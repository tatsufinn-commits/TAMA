## 3. Key Definitions (verified — cross-checked with RAW + official glossaries)

- **Cultural Property** — "all products of human creativity by which a people and a nation reveal their identity," including churches, schools, heritage structures, movable/immovable, tangible/intangible.
- **Important Cultural Property (ICP)** — cultural property with exceptional historical/cultural/artistic/scientific significance; **works by a National Artist or Manlilikha ng Bayan are automatically ICPs** unless declared a National Cultural Treasure.
- **National Cultural Treasure (NCT)** — unique cultural property of outstanding value, officially declared by the pertinent cultural agency.
- **Heritage Zone** — historically/anthropologically/archaeologically significant areas declared by the National Museum and/or NHCP.
- **PRECUP (Philippine Registry of Cultural Property)** — the national inventory (§14); mandatory registration for NCTs, landmarks, shrines, ICPs, heritage zones; voluntary for private owners.
- **World Heritage Sites** — conservation managed in collaboration with the UNESCO National Commission (§6).

## 4. Architecture Relevance (verified from structure)

- **Built heritage** is explicitly within scope: churches, government buildings, houses of ancestry, traditional dwellings, bridges, lighthouses, etc.
- **Conservation** (per the Act's definitions) includes preservation, restoration, reconstruction, protection, adaptation — the vocabulary of heritage-adaptive-reuse studio work.
- Heritage Zones (Vigan, Intramuros contexts) impose design constraints — links to Planning and studio.
