## 1. KEY PROVISIONS OF RA 9266

* **Section 20 (Seal and Use of Seal):** Only registered and licensed architects shall sign and seal architectural documents (plans, specifications, structural architectural layouts).
* **Section 33 (Ownership of Plans):** Drawings, specifications, and related contract documents prepared by an architect are instruments of professional service and remain the **intellectual property of the architect**, whether the project is executed or not.
* **Foreign Architects (Sec. 38):** Foreign architects must work in joint venture / association with a registered Filipino Registered and Licensed Architect (RLA).
