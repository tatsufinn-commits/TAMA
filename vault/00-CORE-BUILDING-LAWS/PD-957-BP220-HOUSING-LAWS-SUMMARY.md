## 1. PD 957 — Subdivision and Condominium Buyers' Protective Decree (1976)

**Purpose (verbatim):** "regulating the sale of subdivision lots and condominiums, providing penalties for violations thereof" — protecting buyers from developers who fail to deliver titles, roads, drainage, water systems, etc.

**Key verified definitions (Sec. 1, excerpts):**
- **Subdivision project** — a tract of registered land partitioned primarily for residential purposes into individual lots, offered for sale; includes residential/commercial/industrial/recreational areas, open spaces, community/public areas.
- **Subdivision lot** — any lot (residential, commercial, industrial, recreational) in a subdivision project.
- **Complex subdivision plan** — a plan of registered land wherein a street, passageway, or open space is delineated.
- **Condominium project** — entire parcel divided primarily for residential purposes into condominium units, including all structures.
- **Condominium unit** — part intended for independent use/ownership, including rooms/spaces in one or more floors + accessories.
- **Owner / Developer** — registered owner of the land; the person who develops/improves the project for the owner.

**Key verified requirement:** **Registration and License to Sell** — developers must secure registration/license before selling subdivision lots or condominium units (Section 5 area, per RAW).

**Exam relevance:** definition items; developer obligations (roads, drainage, sewerage, water, lighting); license-to-sell requirement; buyer protections.

## 2. BP 220 — Economic & Socialized Housing Standards (1985)

**Purpose (verbatim):** "…development of economic and socialized housing projects, primarily by the private sector in order to make available adequate economic and socialized housing units for average and low-income earners in urban and rural areas…"

**Key verified content:**
- Establishes standards for **economic and socialized housing** (distinct from PD 957 regular subdivisions).
- **Socialized housing** defined as units within the affordability range of low-income earners (per RAW text).
- **ANNEX A — Rules and Standards for Economic and Socialized Housing** contains the technical standards (lot sizes, road widths, open spaces — e.g., "2 living units up to a minimum…", "up to a minimum distance of…" per RAW lines).
- References PD 957 ("otherwise known as the Subdivision and Condominium Buyers' Protective Decree") and PD 1216 ("Defining Open Space in Residential Subdivision").

**Exam relevance:** distinction between BP 220 (economic/socialized) vs PD 957 (regular subdivision/condo); minimum standards for socialized housing projects; open-space requirements.
