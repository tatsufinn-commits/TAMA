## 3. Key Administrative Facts (verified)

- Enforcement: **Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP)** via the Chief, BFP (per §5).
- Fire safety clearances / Fire Safety Inspection Certificates (FSIC) are the operational permit instruments (IRR-level).
- §8 lists prohibited acts — good true/false and identification material.
