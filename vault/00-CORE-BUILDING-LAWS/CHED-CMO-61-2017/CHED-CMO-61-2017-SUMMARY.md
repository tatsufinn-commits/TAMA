## 1. Verified Facts (from acquired sample curriculum & registry)

- BS Architecture is governed nationally by **CMO No. 61, s. 2017** (PSG) — confirmed via CHED's official CMO registry and a 2025 peer-published policy analysis of Articles IV–V.
- The **sample curriculum totals 218 credit units**: 13 units mathematics, 9 units natural sciences, 18 units basic engineering courses.
- Minimum professional requirements: **≥44 professional courses (115 units)**, **3 specialization courses (9 units)**, and a **3-unit correlation course** taken in the final year.
- HEIs (like Mapúa) have **flexibility in curriculum design** provided program outcomes are achieved — i.e., Mapúa's actual curriculum is its own implementation; the sample is a national template, NOT Mapúa's curriculum.
- Course families in the sample: Architectural Design 1–9 (studio), Architectural Visual Communications 1–5, Theory of Architecture 1–2, History of Architecture 1–4, Building Technology 1–5, Building Utilities 1–3, Structures chain (Statics → Strength → Theory of Structures → Steel & Timber → Architectural Structures), Planning 1–3, Tropical Design, Housing, Professional Practice 1–3, Research Methods, Specializations, Correlation Course.
