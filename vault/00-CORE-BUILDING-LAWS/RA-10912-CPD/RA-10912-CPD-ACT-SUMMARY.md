## 1. What RA 10912 Establishes (summary)

- **Mandatory CPD** for all registered and licensed professionals under PRC regulation (including architects).
- CPD is a **precondition for renewal of the Professional Identification Card (PIC)**.
- CPD credit units are earned through: formal learning, non-formal learning, informal learning, self-directed learning, professional work experience, and other PRC-recognized activities.
- The **CPD Council per profession** (for architecture: in coordination with the PRBoA and UAP as the accredited professional organization) designs the CPD program.
- Links to RA 9266 §28: the Architecture Act already required CPD for architects; RA 10912 provides the unified national framework.
