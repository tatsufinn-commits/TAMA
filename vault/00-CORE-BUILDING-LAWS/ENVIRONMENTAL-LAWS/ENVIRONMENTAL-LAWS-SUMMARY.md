## 1. PD 1586 — Environmental Impact Statement System (1978)

**What it establishes:** The Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) System — projects/undertakings (public or private) that significantly affect the quality of the environment must secure an **Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC)** from the DENR (EMB) before construction.
- Scope: "environmentally critical projects" + projects in "environmentally critical areas" (per Proclamation 2146 and DENR rules).
- **Architecture relevance:** major building/development projects require ECC as part of pre-design/permitting; site selection must consider EIS requirements. Program Outcome B (planning solutions in ecological balance).

## 2. RA 8749 — Philippine Clean Air Act (1999)

**What it establishes:** Comprehensive air pollution control policy.
- DENR (EMB) implements National Air Quality Standards; pollution from stationary sources (factories, buildings' boilers/incinerators) and mobile sources regulated.
- **Architecture relevance:** building ventilation design, exhaust/stack requirements, no-burn policies, ambient air quality in site selection; relates to BU (mechanical/ventilation) and planning.

## 3. RA 9275 — Philippine Clean Water Act (2004)

**What it establishes:** Comprehensive water quality management; protection of water bodies; sewage/industrial wastewater treatment standards.
- **Architecture relevance:** building sanitary/sewage design (septic systems, wastewater treatment, STP for larger projects), stormwater management, rainwater harvesting context; connects to BU1 plumbing/sanitary (AR153P) and site drainage (Planning 1).

## 4. RA 9003 — Ecological Solid Waste Management Act (2000)

**What it establishes:** National solid waste management framework; segregation, collection, recycling; **Material Recovery Facilities (MRF)** at barangay level; prohibition of open dumps (closure/upgrading).
- **Architecture relevance:** building waste management provisions (chutes, storage rooms, MRF space in developments), sustainable design, planning for waste infrastructure; green-building tie-ins.

## 5. RA 10587 — Environmental Planning Act (2013)

**What it establishes:** Regulates the **practice of environmental planning** (repealed PD 1308); creates the Professional Regulatory Board of Environmental Planning; defines scope of environmental planning practice.
- **Architecture relevance:** clarifies the boundary between architecture and environmental planning practice; CLUP/zoning work falls under environmental planners; architects work with planners on land-use; ALE planning cluster context.

## 6. RA 10754 — Magna Carta of Persons with Disability (2016, expanding benefits)

**What it establishes:** Expands benefits/privileges of PWDs (RA 7277 as amended): 20% discount on certain goods/services, VAT exemption, etc.
- **Architecture relevance:** complements **BP 344** accessibility requirements (design context); PWD rights framework; exam items pairing BP 344 with RA 10754 benefits.
