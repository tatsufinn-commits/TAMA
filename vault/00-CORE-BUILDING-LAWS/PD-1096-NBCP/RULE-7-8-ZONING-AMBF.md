## 1. CORE DEFINITIONS & FORMULAS

* **TLA (Total Lot Area):** Total horizontal area of the lot in square meters.
* **AMBF (Allowable Maximum Building Footprint):** The maximum ground area a building may occupy.
  $$\text{AMBF} = \text{TLA} - \text{TOSL}$$
* **TOSL (Total Open Space within Lot):**
  $$\text{TOSL} = \text{USA (Unpaved Surface Area)} + \text{ISA (Impervious Surface Area)}$$
* **PSO (Percentage of Site Occupancy):**
  $$\text{PSO} = \frac{\text{AMBF}}{\text{TLA}} \times 100\%$$
* **TGFA (Total Gross Floor Area):**
  $$\text{TGFA} = \text{TLA} \times \text{FLAR (Floor to Lot Area Ratio)}$$
