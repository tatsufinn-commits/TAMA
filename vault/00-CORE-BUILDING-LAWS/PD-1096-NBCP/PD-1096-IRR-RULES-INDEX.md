## 2. Verified — What "Referral Codes" means (Rule I / Sec. 103)

> "The applicable and consistent provisions of the allied professional codes and other government agency codes as approved by the DPWH Secretary shall serve as the **referral codes** of PD 1096 and this IRR." — IRR Sec. 103 (RAW line 637–639)

- Referral codes = the technical/professional codes **supplementary to the Code** (definition at RAW line 718): the structural, electrical, mechanical, plumbing, fire and other codes approved by the DPWH Secretary.
- **Exam importance:** PD 1096 does NOT stand alone — every major technical number referenced by the NBCP comes from a referral code (NSCP, PEC, PMC, Plumbing Code, Fire Code). Questions that appear to be "NBCP questions" often test referral-code numbers.
- The DPWH Secretary approves changes/amendments and incorporation of new referral codes (RAW line 776–779).
