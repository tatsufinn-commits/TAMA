## 2. VERIFIED — Supporting formulas & measurement rules (Rule VIII)

- **PSO** = Percentage of Site Occupancy; **measurement taken at ground level**, exclusive of courts, yards, and light wells (Sec. 802/803).
- Courts/yards/light wells measured clear of projections EXCEPT roof leaders, wall copings, sills, or steel fire escapes **not exceeding 1.20 m in width** (Sec. 802.2).
- Existing buildings must be included in PSO computation for additions (Sec. 803.2).
- Where PSO conflicts with light/ventilation provisions → **the lesser building footprint prevails** (Sec. 803.2).
- **Parking slot (average automobile): 2.50 m × 5.00 m** (Rule VII, verified — RAW line 6243).

## 3. VERIFIED — Abutment / firewall rules by residential zone (Rule VIII, PDF p.125)

| Zone | Abutments allowed |
|---|---|
| R-1 | **None** at any property line (any lot type/location) |
| R-2 | One (1) side only; no firewalls/abutments on front & rear property lines |
| R-3 | Two (2) sides, or one side + rear; none on front property line |
| R-4 | Two (2) sides only; none on front & rear |
| R-5 | Two (2) sides, or one side + rear; none on front |
| C-3 | Two (2) sides, or one side + rear; none on front |
