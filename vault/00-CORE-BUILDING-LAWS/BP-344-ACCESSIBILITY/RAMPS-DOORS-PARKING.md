# BP 344 — ACCESSIBILITY LAW SUMMARY & METRICS
**Authority:** Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 (An Act to Enhance the Mobility of Disabled Persons)  
**High-Yield Score:** 9.5 / 10  

---

## 1. PEDESTRIAN ACCESSIBILITY RAMPS (RULE II)

* **Maximum Ramp Slope:** $1:12$ ($8.33\%$).
* **Minimum Clear Width:** $1.20\text{m}$.
* **Maximum Horizontal Run per Ramp:** $6.00\text{m}$ (A level landing of at least $1.50\text{m}$ length is required every $6.00\text{m}$ of run).
* **Handrail Height:** Dual handrails required at $0.70\text{m}$ and $0.90\text{m}$ above ramp floor.
* **Curbs:** $0.10\text{m}$ ($100\text{mm}$) minimum curb height along ramp edges to prevent wheels from slipping.

---

## 2. DOORS, CORRIDORS & WASHROOMS

* **Minimum Clear Door Width:** $0.80\text{m}$ ($800\text{mm}$), measured between door face and stop with door open $90^\circ$.
* **Doorknobs / Latches:** Lever-type handles preferred, mounted $0.82\text{m}$ to $1.06\text{m}$ above floor ($0.90\text{m}$ preferred).
* **Accessible Water Closet Stall:** Minimum $1.70\text{m} \times 1.80\text{m}$ ($1.50\text{m} \times 1.50\text{m}$ minimum clear wheelchair turning radius).
* **Grab Bars:** $0.80\text{m}$ height above floor level.
