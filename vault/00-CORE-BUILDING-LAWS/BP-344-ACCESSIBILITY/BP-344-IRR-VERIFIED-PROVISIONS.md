## 1. The Law Proper (verified quotes)

- **Coverage:** buildings, institutions, establishments and public utilities must install facilities enhancing mobility of disabled persons — sidewalks, ramps, railings, etc.; permit/license issuance conditioned on compliance.
- **Accessible parking:** owners/operators must "reserve sufficient and suitable space" for disabled persons.
- **Government living accommodations:** **10%** of units must be accessible and fully usable by disabled persons (fraction > ½ counts as one unit).
- **Penalties (§4):** imprisonment 1 month – 1 year, or fine P2,000–P5,000, or both (corporations: responsible officer charged).
- **IRR rule of thumb (verified):** accessible parking slots "should be located as close as possible to building entrances."
