## 1. PRESTRESSED CONCRETE (PRE-TENSIONING vs POST-TENSIONING)

* **Pre-tensioning:** Tendons (high-strength steel strands) are tensioned *before* concrete is poured (precast yards, bridge girders, hollow-core slabs).
* **Post-tensioning:** Tendons enclosed in ducts are stressed *after* concrete reaches sufficient compressive strength (cast-in-place high-rise slabs, long-span transfer girders).
  * **Minimum Concrete Strength at Transfer ($f'_{ci}$):** At least $20.5\text{ MPa}$ ($3,000\text{ psi}$) per ACI 318.
  * **Grouting:** Non-shrink cementitious grout injected into ducts for bonded post-tensioned systems.

---

## 2. STRUCTURAL STEEL PROFILES & CONNECTIONS

* **Wide Flange ($W$-Shape):** Standard column and girder profile (e.g. $W 14 \times 90 = 14\text{ inch nominal depth}, 90\text{ lbs/linear ft}$).
* **Connection Types:**
  * **Shear Connection (Flexible / Simple):** Connects web only; transfers vertical shear, allows minor rotation.
  * **Moment Connection (Rigid):** Connects web and flanges with full-penetration groove welds; resists seismic/lateral rotation.
* **Standard Bolt Grades:** ASTM A325 (High-strength carbon steel) and ASTM A490 (Alloy steel for heavy seismic joints).
