## 1. SPACE PROGRAMMING METHODOLOGY

* **Net Floor Area (NFA):** Usable assignable area occupied directly by occupants or activities (desks, beds, classrooms).
* **Gross Floor Area (GFA):** Total floor area including circulation corridors, wall thicknesses, mechanical shafts, and washrooms.
* **Circulation Multiplier Rule of Thumb:**
  * Offices / Commercial: Add **$20\% \text{ to } 25\%$** for circulation.
  * Hospitals / Complex Institutional: Add **$30\% \text{ to } 40\%$** for circulation.
  * Residential: Add **$15\% \text{ to } 20\%$** for circulation.

## 2. SITE ZONING & CIRCULATION MATRIX

* **Vehicular vs. Pedestrian Segregation:** Pedestrian walkways must never cross primary vehicular loading/delivery zones at blind corners.
* **Drop-Off vs. Service Loading:**
  * Main Public Entry: Drop-off bay facing primary RROW with minimum $6.00\text{m}$ clear driveway.
  * Service / Delivery Bay: Located at the rear or secondary street edge, shielded from public view.
* **Sun & Wind Orientation (Philippine Tropical Context):**
  * **Habitable Spaces / Classrooms:** Orient long axis along **East-West** so windows face **North and South** (avoids direct solar heat gain).
  * **Service Core / Toilets / Elevators:** Place on **West Façade** to act as a thermal buffer against harsh afternoon sun.
  * **Prevailing Breezes:** *Amihan* (Northeast, Nov–Feb) and *Habagat* (Southwest, June–Oct).
