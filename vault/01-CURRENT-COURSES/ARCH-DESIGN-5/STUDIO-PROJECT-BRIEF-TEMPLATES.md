## 1. THE STANDARD BRIEF FORMAT (use for every exercise)

```
PROJECT TITLE
├── 1. CLIENT & PURPOSE        (who, why, budget class)
├── 2. PROGRAM / SPACES        (space list w/ approx. areas + adjacencies)
├── 3. SITE                    (given site: dimensions, zoning, context)
├── 4. CONSTRAINTS             (budget, codes, climate, client quirks)
├── 5. DELIVERABLES            (bubble diagram, adjacency matrix, parti, massing, plan)
└── 6. EVALUATION CRITERIA     (concept clarity, function, climate response, code, presentation)
```

**Programming tools to require in every exercise:** bubble diagram → adjacency matrix → space program table (space, area, qty, total, net→gross w/ efficiency) → parti statement.

## 2. BRIEF SET A — AD2/AD3 LEVEL (introductory: small, single-use)

### Brief A1: "The Reading Nook Café"
- **Client:** a young couple; modest budget.
- **Program:** café (20 seats indoor + 10 outdoor), small kitchen (prep-only), restrooms (2 — one PWD per BP 344), storage, office nook.
- **Site:** 12 m × 20 m corner lot, C-2 zone; RROW 12 m; inside lot; tropical climate.
- **Constraints:** PSO per C-2 (verify Table VIII.1); BP 344 accessibility; natural ventilation priority.
- **Deliverables:** bubble diagram, adjacency matrix, program table, parti, schematic plan (1:100).
- **Evaluation:** functional relationships, climate response, code compliance.

### Brief A2: "The Artist's Studio-House"
- **Client:** a painter needing live + work.
- **Program:** living (2 BR), studio (north-light), gallery corner, storage, small garden.
- **Site:** 10 m × 25 m, R-2 zone; single-family; 2-storey max.
- **Constraints:** R-2 PSO/ISA per Table VIII.1; front yard setbacks; north-light orientation.
- **Deliverables:** same set + section showing light.
- **Evaluation:** light/ventilation strategy; zoning compliance.

## 3. BRIEF SET B — AD5 LEVEL (space planning emphasis)

### Brief B1: "The Neighborhood Clinic"
- **Client:** a midwife/nurse practitioner + 2 staff.
- **Program:** waiting, 3 consult/exam rooms, treatment room, records, pharmacy counter, staff room, restrooms, wheelchair access throughout (BP 344).
- **Site:** 15 m × 30 m, C-1 zone (institutional-adjacent).
- **Constraints:** BP 344 (doors ≥0.80 m, ramps ≤1:12), PD 1096 occupancy, privacy/patient flow (infection control adjacency), PSO per C-1.
- **Deliverables:** adjacency matrix (strict: exam→treatment proximity), bubble diagram, program table, 2 massing alternatives, SD plan.
- **Evaluation:** circulation logic (patient vs staff paths), accessibility compliance, code.

### Brief B2: "The 3-Bedroom Tropical House (Space Planning)"
- **Client:** family of 5; active lifestyle; tight budget.
- **Program:** 3 BR, 2 baths, open living-dining-kitchen, service area, carport, lanai/veranda.
- **Site:** 8 m × 20 m (narrow), R-2; E–W long axis.
- **Constraints:** narrow-lot planning (stacked circulation), E–W orientation shading, cross-ventilation (no AC reliance), R-2 PSO.
- **Deliverables:** program table w/ net→gross, adjacency, parti, plan + section.
- **Evaluation:** spatial efficiency on narrow lot; passive design integration (ties TROPICAL file).

## 4. BRIEF SET C — AD6/AD7 LEVEL (site + community emphasis)

### Brief C1: "The Urban Pocket Park + Community Pavilion"
- **Client:** city LGU (public project).
- **Program:** pocket park (green, play, seating), community pavilion (multi-purpose hall, storage, restrooms), bike racks, PWD access.
- **Site:** 40 m × 50 m vacant block, mixed-use zone; adjacent to market & school.
- **Constraints:** PD 1586 ECC check (public project scale), RA 9003 (waste), zoning, heritage-zone check (RA 10066), tropical landscape (shade trees).
- **Deliverables:** site analysis (sun/wind/access), 3 site-plan alternatives, landscape plan, pavilion schematic.
- **Evaluation:** community fit, environmental design, accessibility, program.

### Brief C2: "The Socialized Housing Cluster (BP 220)"
- **Client:** an NGO + LGU; very tight budget (socialized housing per BP 220).
- **Program:** 12–20 housing units (rowhouse/duplex), community open space, day-care, sari-sari store, pathways, parking (minimal), MRF (RA 9003).
- **Site:** 60 m × 80 m, R-3/socialized zone.
- **Constraints:** BP 220 standards (lot sizes, roads — verify current Annex A), PD 957 subdivision rules, drainage/flood, typhoon resilience.
- **Deliverables:** site plan, unit plans, section, infrastructure concept (water/sanitary/storm).
- **Evaluation:** density + affordability + dignity; code compliance; climate.
