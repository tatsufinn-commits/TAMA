## 1. CENTRAL HVAC SYSTEMS (CHILLERS & ALL-AIR SYSTEMS)

* **Vapor Compression Cycle:** Evaporator $\rightarrow$ Compressor $\rightarrow$ Condenser $\rightarrow$ Expansion Valve.
* **Cooling Load Metric:** **1 Ton of Refrigeration (TR) = 12,000 BTU/hr = 3.517 kW**.
* **Air Handling Unit (AHU):** Conditions and circulates air; requires minimum $15\text{ to }20\text{ CFM}$ outdoor fresh air per person.
* **Chilled Water Loop:** Chilled water at $7^\circ\text{C}$ ($45^\circ\text{F}$) supplied to AHU coils; returns at $12^\circ\text{C}$ ($55^\circ\text{F}$).

## 2. ARCHITECTURAL ACOUSTICS & REVERBERATION TIME ($RT_{60}$)

* **Sabine Formula for Reverberation Time:**
  $$RT_{60} = \frac{0.161 \cdot V}{A}$$
  * $V =$ Room volume in cubic meters ($m^3$).
  * $A = \sum S_i \cdot \alpha_i =$ Total sound absorption in Sabins ($m^2$).
  * $\alpha =$ Noise Reduction Coefficient (NRC) / Sound Absorption Coefficient.
* **Optimum $RT_{60}$ Targets:**
  * Speech / Lecture Halls: **$0.8\text{ to }1.2\text{ seconds}$**
  * Symphonic Concert Halls: **$1.8\text{ to }2.2\text{ seconds}$**
