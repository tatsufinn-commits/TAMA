## 2. CANONICAL MOVEMENTS, MASTERS & THE PHILIPPINE MATRIX

### 2.1 The Master Pioneers of Modernism
* **Walter Gropius & The Bauhaus (Dessau, 1925):** Total work of art (*Gesamtkunstwerk*), fusion of art, craft, and industrial technology; steel-framed glass curtain walls.
* **Ludwig Mies van der Rohe (1886–1969):** *"Less is more"* / *"God is in the details"*; master of structural clarity and universal space (**Barcelona Pavilion**, **Farnsworth House**, **Seagram Building**).
* **Le Corbusier (1887–1965):** The **5 Points of Modern Architecture** (*Pilotis, Roof Garden, Free Plan, Ribbon Windows, Free Façade*) demonstrated in **Villa Savoye** (1929) and later expressive raw concrete (*Béton Brut*) in **Notre Dame du Haut, Ronchamp** (1954) and Chandigarh.
* **Frank Lloyd Wright (1867–1959):** **Organic Architecture** (buildings growing naturally out of their site); Prairie Houses (**Robie House**), **Fallingwater** (1939), **Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum** (1959).

### 2.2 Philippine Post-War Modernism & National Artists
* **Leandro V. Locsin (1928–1994):** Master of the massive **"Floating Mass"** in monumental poured-in-place concrete:
  * **Cultural Center of the Philippines (CCP Main Theater):** $13.5\text{m}$ cantilevered concrete block floating over a curved travertine podium.
  * **Church of the Holy Sacrifice (UP Diliman):** First thin-shell concrete dome in the Philippines ($76\text{mm}$ thickness, $29.3\text{m}$ diameter) with unbraced perimeter columns.
  * **Philippine International Convention Center (PICC)** and Istana Nurul Iman (Brunei).
* **Francisco "Bobby" Mañosa (1931–2019):** Master of **Philippine Neo-Vernacular Regionalism**:
  * **The Coconut Palace (Tahanang Pilipino):** $70\%$ constructed from the coconut tree (coconut lumber, shell inlays, husk fibers).
  * **San Miguel Corporation Complex (Mandaluyong):** Stepped terraced massing inspired by the **Banaue Rice Terraces** featuring extensive exterior greenery and deep horizontal solar shading eaves.
* **Pablo S. Antonio (1901–1975):** Master of Philippine Art Deco and tropical climate cross-ventilation (**Far Eastern University - FEU Campus Complex**).

## 3. SOCRATIC EXAM SIMULATION (BOARD-STYLE SCENARIOS)

#### Question 1 (Leandro Locsin's Iconic Structural Masterpiece):
The Church of the Holy Sacrifice in UP Diliman, designed by National Artist Leandro V. Locsin and structural engineer Alfredo Juinio, is famous in Philippine architectural history as the country's first circular church and first monumental application of what structural technology?
- A) Post-tensioned two-way flat plate slab
- B) Thin-shell concrete dome with open perimeter ring ventilation
- C) Space frame tetrahedron truss
- D) Vierendeel steel transfer girder
> **Correct Answer: B**  
> **Distractor Trap Analysis**:
> * The **Church of the Holy Sacrifice** (consecrated in 1955) was the first thin-shell concrete dome in the Philippines, constructed with a shell thickness of just $76\text{mm}$ ($3"$) spanning nearly $30.0\text{m}$ without interior column supports. It also represents a historic collaboration between 4 National Artists (Locsin for Architecture, Vicente Manansala and Ang Kiukok for Stations of the Cross murals, Napoleon Abueva for the Cross, and Arturo Luz for the River of Life terrazzo floor).
