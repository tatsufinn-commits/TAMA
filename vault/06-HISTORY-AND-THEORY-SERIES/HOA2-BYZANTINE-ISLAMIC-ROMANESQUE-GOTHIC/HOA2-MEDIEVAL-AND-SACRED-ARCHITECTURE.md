## 2. CANONICAL PERIOD TYPOLOGIES & STRUCTURAL ANATOMY

### 2.1 Byzantine Architecture & Pendentive Mechanics
* **Pendentive vs Squinch:**
  * **Pendentive:** A triangular curved spherical segment transitioning a circular dome onto a **square floor plan** (exemplified by **Hagia Sophia, Istanbul**, 537 AD by Isidore of Miletus & Anthemius of Tralles).
  * **Squinch:** An arch or corbeled bracket placed diagonally across the upper corners of a square room to support an octagonal dome base.

### 2.2 Islamic Sacred Architecture & Mosque Anatomy
* **Core Components of the Mosque (*Masjid*):**
  * **Qibla Wall:** The sacred interior wall oriented toward the Kaaba in Mecca.
  * **Mihrab:** Semicircular prayer niche recessed into the Qibla wall indicating the direction of Mecca.
  * **Minbar:** Elevated stepped pulpit from which the Friday sermon (*Khutbah*) is delivered.
  * **Minaret:** Slender tower from which the call to prayer (*Adhan*) is chanted.
  * **Muqarnas:** Corbeled honeycomb vaulting and ornamental stalactite niches.
  * **Horseshoe & Multifoil Arches:** Characteristic arch typologies of Moorish Spain (**The Great Mosque of Cordoba**, **The Alhambra in Granada**).

### 2.3 Romanesque vs. Gothic Comparative Architecture Matrix
| Architectural Feature | Romanesque Architecture (c. 900–1150) | Gothic Architecture (c. 1140–1500) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dominant Structural Arch** | Semicircular (Round) Roman Arch | **Pointed (Ogival) Arch** (Generates less lateral outward thrust) |
| **Vaulting System** | Heavy continuous Barrel Vaults & Groin Vaults | **Ribbed Groin Vaults** with diagonal and transverse ribs |
| **Lateral Thrust Resisting System**| Massive thick masonry walls with heavy exterior pilasters | **Detached Flying Buttresses** with weighted stone pinnacles |
| **Interior Illumination & Openings**| Small, narrow, deeply recessed window slits (Dark, fortress-like) | **Towering Stained-Glass Windows & Rose Windows** (Light as Divine Grace) |
| **Plan Configuration** | Latin Cross with wide radiating ambulatory chapels for pilgrims | Soaring Latin Cross with tripartite elevation (Arcade, Triforium, Clerestory) |
| **Iconic Masterpieces** | **Saint-Sernin (Toulouse)**, Durham Cathedral, Speyer Cathedral | **Abbey of Saint-Denis** (Suger), **Chartres Cathedral**, **Notre Dame de Paris** |

## 3. SOCRATIC EXAM SIMULATION (BOARD-STYLE SCENARIOS)

#### Question 1 (Gothic Structural Trinity):
The revolutionary skeletal stone construction system of High Gothic cathedrals that allowed exterior load-bearing walls to be replaced by towering stained-glass screens was achieved by the simultaneous synthesis of which three structural elements?
- A) Barrel vaults, squinches, and thick masonry piers
- B) Pointed arches, ribbed vaults, and flying buttresses
- C) Pendentives, horseshoe arches, and wooden tie beams
- D) Post-tensioned tie rods, corbeled lintels, and coffered domes
> **Correct Answer: B**  
> **Distractor Trap Analysis**:
> * The Gothic structural revolution is defined by the **Pointed Arch** (reduces lateral thrust and allows spans of unequal widths to reach uniform crown heights) + **Ribbed Vault** (channels roof loads along distinct line vectors) + **Flying Buttress** (transfers outward lateral thrust away from walls to external piers).
