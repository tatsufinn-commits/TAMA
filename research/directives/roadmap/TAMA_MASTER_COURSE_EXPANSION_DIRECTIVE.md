# 2. THE 7 TARGET COURSES SYLLABUS DECOMPOSITION

Here is the exact 14-week academic scope required for each of the missing/under-developed courses:

### 1. Building Technology 2 (AR162-1P / BT2)
* **Scope:** Small Building Construction Drawings, Wood Framing, Masonry, Steel Trusses & Shallow Foundations.
* **14-Week Topics:**
  * Weeks 1–2: Standard Working Drawing Conventions, Sheet Layouts, Line Weights, Title Blocks (RA 9266 format).
  * Weeks 3–4: Shallow Foundation Substructures (Isolated, Wall, Combined, Mat Footings, Tie Beams, Excavation).
  * Weeks 5–6: Floor Framing Systems (Slab-on-Fill, Ground Slabs, Suspended One-Way RC Slabs, Timber Joists).
  * Weeks 7–8: Wall Systems & Masonry Detailing (Confined CHB 100mm/150mm, Stiffener Columns, Lintel Beams).
  * Weeks 9–10: Roof Framing Systems (Timber Rafters, Light-Gauge Steel Trusses, Purlin Spacing, Metal Decking).
  * Weeks 11–12: Architectural Openings & Millwork (Wood Panel Doors, Sliding Glass, Casement Windows, Finishes).
  * Weeks 13–14: Complete 1-Storey Working Drawing Package (Architectural, Structural, Electrical, Plumbing coordination).

### 2. Strength of Materials & Structures 2 (MEC32 / STRUC2)
* **Scope:** Mechanics of Deformable Bodies, Flexural Stresses, Horizontal Shear, Deflection & Column Buckling.
* **14-Week Topics:**
  * Weeks 1–2: Direct Stress & Strain, Hooke's Law, Modulus of Elasticity ($E$), Thermal Stress ($\delta = \alpha L \Delta T$).
  * Weeks 3–4: Thin-Walled Pressure Vessels ($p r / t$) and Torsional Shear Stress in Circular Shafts ($\tau = T r / J$).
  * Weeks 5–6: Shear and Moment Equations & Diagrams for Determinately Supported Beams ($dV/dx = -w, dM/dx = V$).
  * Weeks 7–8: Pure Bending & The Flexure Formula ($\sigma = My/I = M/S$), Section Modulus optimization.
  * Weeks 9–10: Transverse Horizontal Shear Stress in Beams ($\tau = VQ/Ib$), Shear Flow in Built-up Girders ($q = VQ/I$).
  * Weeks 11–12: Beam Deflection Derivations (Double Integration, Moment-Area Theorems, Standard Superposition Cases).
  * Weeks 13–14: Column Buckling Mechanics (Euler's Critical Load $P_{cr} = \pi^2 E I / (KL)^2$, Slenderness Ratio $KL/r$).

### 3. Professional Practice 2 (AR182-1 / PP2)
* **Scope:** SPP Documents 201 to 208, Architect's Professional Fees, Methods of Compensation, Project Delivery & Arbitration.
* **14-Week Topics:**
  * Weeks 1–2: SPP 201 (Pre-Design Services: Feasibility Studies, Site Selection, Space Programming, Heritage Conservation).
  * Weeks 3–4: SPP 202 (Regular Design Services: Schematic Design 15%, Design Development 20%, Contract Docs 40%, Bidding 5%, Construction Administration 20%).
  * Weeks 5–6: SPP 203 (Specialized Architectural Services: Interior Design, Acoustic Design, Landscape, Lighting, Heritage).
  * Weeks 7–8: SPP 204A & 204B (Full-Time Construction Supervision & Construction Management).
  * Weeks 9–10: SPP 205, 206, 207, 208 (Post-Construction, Comprehensive Architectural Services, Design-Build, Competitions).
  * Weeks 11–12: Methods of Compensation (Percentage of Project Construction Cost, Multiple of Direct Personnel Expense - MDPE, Professional Fee Plus Expenses, Lump Sum / Fixed Fee).
  * Weeks 13–14: Construction Dispute Resolution (Construction Industry Arbitration Commission - CIAC, Executive Order 1008).

### 4. Building Utilities 2 (BU2: Electrical & Mechanical Systems)
* **Scope:** Power Generation & Distribution, Philippine Electrical Code (PEC), Artificial Lighting, Transformers & Mechanical Services.
* **14-Week Topics:**
  * Weeks 1–2: Electrical Physics Fundamentals: Single-Phase vs Three-Phase ($230\text{V} / 400\text{V}$), Ohm's Law, Power Factor ($PF$).
  * Weeks 3–4: PEC Branch Circuit Design: Wire Gauge Sizing (THHN/THWN Ampacities), Conduit Sizing, Overcurrent Protection.
  * Weeks 5–6: Service Entrance & Panelboard Sizing: Connected Load vs Demand Load Calculations (PEC Table 2.20.42).
  * Weeks 7–8: Emergency Power Systems: Standby Diesel Generators, Automatic Transfer Switches (ATS), Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS).
  * Weeks 9–10: Illumination Engineering & Lighting Design: Lumen Method ($E = \Phi \cdot CU \cdot LLF / A$), CCT, CRI, Glare Control.
  * Weeks 11–12: Building Transportation & Mechanical Rooms: Elevator Traffic Planning ($HC_{5min}\%$, $RTT$), Escalator 30° Incline.
  * Weeks 13–14: Fire Protection & Life Safety Integration: Fire Alarm and Detection Systems (FDAS), Smoke Dampers, Standpipes.

### 5. History of Architecture 2 (HOA2: Byzantine, Islamic, Romanesque & Gothic)
* **14-Week Topics:**
  * Weeks 1–3: Byzantine Architecture (Hagia Sophia, Pendentives vs Squinches, Greek Cross Plan, Mosaic Iconography).
  * Weeks 4–6: Islamic Architecture (The Mosque Typology: Minaret, Mihrab, Qibla Wall, Minbar, Muqarnas, Horseshoe Arches, Alhambra).
  * Weeks 7–9: Romanesque Architecture (Massive Masonry, Barrel & Groin Vaults, Radiating Chapels, Santiago de Compostela).
  * Weeks 10–12: Gothic Architecture Evolution (Early, High, Rayonnant, Flamboyant; Pointed Arches, Flying Buttresses, Rib Vaults, Rose Windows, St-Denis, Chartres, Notre Dame de Paris, Salisbury Cathedral).
  * Weeks 13–14: Comparative Structural Analysis & Spatial Synthesis (Gothic Light vs Romanesque Darkness).

### 6. History of Architecture 3 (HOA3: Renaissance, Baroque, Rococo & 19th Century)
* **14-Week Topics:**
  * Weeks 1–3: Early & High Renaissance (Humanism, Golden Proportions, Brunelleschi's Florence Dome, Alberti, Bramante's Tempietto).
  * Weeks 4–6: Late Renaissance & Mannerism (Michelangelo's Campidoglio & St. Peter's Dome, Andrea Palladio's *Four Books of Architecture* & Villa Rotonda).
  * Weeks 7–9: Baroque & Rococo Architecture (Dynamic Curvature, Bernini's Piazza San Pietro, Borromini's San Carlo, Versailles Palace).
  * Weeks 10–12: Neoclassicism, Beaux-Arts & Revivalism (Panthéon Paris, Karl Friedrich Schinkel, Industrial Iron Structures: Crystal Palace, Eiffel Tower).
  * Weeks 13–14: Early Modern Precursors (Chicago School: Louis Sullivan, Wainwright Building; Art Nouveau: Antoni Gaudí, Victor Horta).

### 7. History of Architecture 4 (HOA4: Modern Movements, Post-Modernism & Philippine Historiography)
* **14-Week Topics:**
  * Weeks 1–3: The Bauhaus & European Modernism (Walter Gropius, Mies van der Rohe's *"Less is more"*, Le Corbusier's 5 Points).
  * Weeks 4–6: Organic Architecture & International Style (Frank Lloyd Wright, Alvar Aalto, Seagram Building, Skidmore Owings & Merrill).
  * Weeks 7–8: Late Modernism, Brutalism & Structural Expressionism (Le Corbusier's Ronchamp & Chandigarh, Paul Rudolph, Kenzo Tange).
  * Weeks 9–10: Post-Modernism, Deconstructivism & High-Tech (Robert Venturi, Michael Graves, Frank Gehry, Zaha Hadid, Renzo Piano).
  * Weeks 11–12: Asian & Southeast Asian Regionalism (Geoffrey Bawa, Minoru Yamasaki, Ken Yeang's Bioclimatic Skyscrapers).
  * Weeks 13–14: Comprehensive Philippine Modernism & Heritage (National Artists: Nakpil, Antonio, Locsin, Mañosa, Zaragoza, Santos; Vernacular Precedents).
