# TAMA — COURSE KNOWLEDGE EXPANSION & RESOURCE ACQUISITION MASTER DIRECTIVE

## MISSION

TAMA's Vault currently contains course entries, but many individual course files have insufficient context and may contain only a small amount of meaningful academic information.

This is now a major knowledge-acquisition problem.

The objective of this mission is to systematically research **every course currently represented in the TAMA Vault**, expand the knowledge contained within each course, and populate the Vault with high-quality supporting educational resources.

This is NOT simply a request to make Markdown files longer.

The objective is to transform each course from:

> **A minimal course entry**

into:

> **A structured, researched, source-aware, interconnected academic knowledge domain supported by real educational resources.**

The final Vault should provide enough context for future TAMA agents to:

- teach the course;
- explain concepts;
- create reviewers;
- generate lessons;
- generate flashcards;
- create quizzes;
- create examinations;
- answer course-specific questions;
- explain difficult concepts;
- connect the course to other subjects;
- support architecture-related applications;
- identify gaps in the student's knowledge.

# 3. DEFINE "SUFFICIENT CONTEXT"

Do not use page count as the primary measure of quality.

A course should be considered adequately contextualized when its knowledge base provides enough information for another AI agent to understand:

- what the course is;
- why the course exists;
- what students are expected to learn;
- what concepts belong to the course;
- how those concepts relate to one another;
- what terminology students need;
- what theories/frameworks are involved;
- what methods or processes are involved;
- what practical skills are involved;
- what examples illustrate the concepts;
- what case studies are relevant;
- how the course relates to architecture;
- where relevant, how the course relates to Philippine practice;
- how the course connects to other courses;
- which sources support the information;
- what knowledge remains missing.

A 10-page file containing filler is not success.

A shorter file containing dense, well-researched knowledge may be sufficient.

The objective is:

> **Useful academic depth, not artificial document length.**

# 20. DISTINGUISH KNOWLEDGE STATUS

Course material should distinguish:

### VERIFIED

Directly supported by reliable evidence.

### SYNTHESIS

A conclusion created by combining multiple sources.

### INTERPRETATION

An academic interpretation.

### UNVERIFIED

Potentially useful information that has not yet been sufficiently confirmed.

### HISTORICAL

Information retained for historical/academic purposes but not necessarily current.

### SUPERSEDED

Information that was once valid but has been replaced.

This is especially important for:

- laws;
- regulations;
- standards;
- university requirements;
- technical specifications.
