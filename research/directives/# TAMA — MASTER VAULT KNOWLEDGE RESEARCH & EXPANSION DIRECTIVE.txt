# CRITICAL RESEARCH PRINCIPLES

### PRINCIPLE 1 — STUDY THE STATUTES & PRIMARY SOURCES, NOT HEARSAY
Do not rely on third-party student blog posts or unverified summaries. When researching building laws, extract data directly from the official Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) of:
* Presidential Decree No. 1096 (National Building Code of the Philippines — 2004 Revised IRR)
* Republic Act No. 9514 (Revised Fire Code of the Philippines of 2008 & 2019 IRR)
* Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 (Accessibility Law & 1994 IRR)
* Republic Act No. 9266 (The Architecture Act of 2004 & PRBoA Resolutions)
* National Structural Code of the Philippines (NSCP 2015 / 7th Edition)
* Revised National Plumbing Code of the Philippines (1999)
* Philippine Electrical Code (PEC 2017)
* UAP Document 301 & Standards of Professional Practice (SPP Document 200 Series)

### PRINCIPLE 2 — ZERO HALLUCINATION ON DIMENSIONS & METRICS
Architectural and building code metrics are legally binding and mathematically non-negotiable.
* If BP 344 mandates a $1.20\text{m}$ clear ramp width, you must never write $1.10\text{m}$.
* If RA 9514 specifies $1.80\text{ sqm per person}$ for classroom occupant load, you must never approximate it as $2.0\text{ sqm}$.
* If NSCP mandates $W_u = 1.2D + 1.6L$, you must never use outdated load factors ($1.4D + 1.7L$) without explicitly clarifying code evolution.

### PRINCIPLE 3 — SOCRATIC DEPTH OVER PASSIVE DEFINITIONS
Do not simply state formulas. Explain the physical engineering mechanism:
* *Why* is the Whitney equivalent stress block depth $a = \frac{A_s f_y}{0.85 f'_c b}$?
* *Why* does a post-tensioned tendon require a minimum concrete transfer strength of $20.5\text{ MPa}$ ($3,000\text{ psi}$)?
* *What happens* to building acoustics when the reverberation time ($RT_{60}$) exceeds $2.2\text{ seconds}$?

### PRINCIPLE 4 — DISTRACTOR TRAP ENGINEERING
Mapúa Departmentals and PRC Board Exams are designed around **calculation traps** and **distractor options**. For every calculation domain, you must identify:
* The common calculation mistakes students make (e.g., forgetting setbacks, confusing TOSL with USA, using gross area instead of net).
* How examiners construct the 3 wrong choices (distractors) to trick unprepared students.

## CLUSTER 1: BUILDING LAWS, CODES & PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE (`vault/01-BUILDING-LAWS-AND-PROFPRAC/`)
* **PD 1096 (National Building Code):**
  * Rule VII & VIII: Table VII.1 Maximum Allowable Percentage of Site Occupancy (PSO), Total Open Space within Lot (TOSL), Unpaved Surface Area (USA), Impervious Surface Area (ISA).
  * Allowable Maximum Building Footprint (AMBF) and Total Gross Floor Area (TGFA) via Floor to Lot Area Ratio (FLAR Table VII.G.1).
  * Road Right-of-Way (RROW) widths, front/side/rear setback matrices per zone (R-1, R-2, R-3, C-1, C-2, C-3, Industrial).
  * Building Height Limit (BHL) and the $1:1.75$ angular plane projection from opposite edge of RROW.
  * Off-street parking slot dimensions (perpendicular $2.50\text{m} \times 5.00\text{m}$, parallel $2.15\text{m} \times 6.00\text{m}$, bus $3.60\text{m} \times 12.00\text{m}$) and ratios per building use.
  * Light and ventilation court dimensions, minimum ceiling heights ($2.70\text{m}$ ground, $2.40\text{m}$ upper, $2.10\text{m}$ non-habitable).
* **RA 9514 (Revised Fire Code of the Philippines):**
  * Section 10.2.5.2 Occupant Load Factors (classrooms $1.80\text{ sqm}$, offices $9.30\text{ sqm}$, mercantile $2.80\text{ sqm}$, assembly $0.65\text{ sqm}$).
  * Egress capacity factors ($0.0076\text{m/person}$ stairways, $0.005\text{m/person}$ level components).
  * Minimum exit stairway widths ($0.91\text{m}$ for $<50$ occupants; $1.12\text{m}$ for $\ge 50$ occupants).
  * Maximum travel distance to exits ($46.00\text{m}$ unsprinklered vs $61.00\text{m}$ sprinklered).
  * Dead-end corridor limits ($6.00\text{m}$ vs $15.00\text{m}$ sprinklered).
  * Standpipe classifications (Class I $64\text{mm}$, Class II $38\text{mm}$, Class III combination).
* **BP 344 (Accessibility Law):**
  * Pedestrian accessibility ramp slope ($1:12$ max / $8.33\%$), $1.20\text{m}$ clear width, $6.00\text{m}$ max run, $1.50\text{m} \times 1.50\text{m}$ landings.
  * Dual handrail heights ($0.70\text{m}$ and $0.90\text{m}$) with $300\text{mm}$ horizontal extensions.
  * Door clear opening width ($0.80\text{m}$ / $800\text{mm}$ min) and lever handle heights ($0.82\text{m}$ to $1.06\text{m}$).
  * Accessible toilet stall dimensions ($1.70\text{m} \times 1.80\text{m}$ min) with $1.50\text{m}$ turning circle and $0.80\text{m}$ grab bars.
  * Accessible PWD parking slots ($3.70\text{m} \times 5.00\text{m}$ with $1.20\text{m}$ walkway).
* **RA 9266 & SPP Document 200 Series:**
  * Scope of practice, ownership of architectural plans (Sec. 33), foreign architect joint-venture rules (Sec. 38).
  * SPP 201 (Pre-Design), SPP 202 (Regular Design Phases), SPP 203 (Specialized), SPP 204A/B (Supervision/Management), SPP 205 (Post-Construction), SPP 206 (Comprehensive), SPP 207 (Design-Build), SPP 208 (Competitions).
  * UAP Document 301: Owner-Architect-Contractor relationships, contract documents, bidding bonds ($2-5\%$), performance bonds ($15-30\%$), maintenance guarantee bond ($10-20\%$ / 1 year period), retention fees ($10\%$).

* **BU1 (Plumbing & Sanitary Systems):** Drainage, Waste, and Vent (DWV) piping networks, National Plumbing Code $2\%$ ($20\text{mm/m}$) horizontal slope for pipes $\le 100\text{mm}$ ($4"$), minimum trap and branch diameters ($75-100\text{mm}$ water closet, $32-38\text{mm}$ lavatory, $50\text{mm}$ floor drain), septic tank digestion ($2/3$) and leaching ($1/3$) chamber volume calculations, roof vent terminals ($2.00\text{m}$ deck / $0.30\text{m}$ slope).

## CLUSTER 5: STRUCTURAL STUDIES & MECHANICS (`vault/05-STRUCTURAL-STUDIES-SERIES/`)
* **STRUC1 (Statics & Equilibrium):** 2D planar static equilibrium equations ($\sum F_x = 0, \sum F_y = 0, \sum M = 0$), support reaction calculations for simply supported, overhanging, and cantilever beams, free-body diagrams (FBD), centroids, second moment of area / moment of inertia ($I = \frac{bh^3}{12}$ for rectangle).
* **STRUC2 (Strength of Materials):** Flexural bending stress formula ($\sigma = \frac{My}{I} = \frac{M}{S}$), elastic section modulus ($S = \frac{bh^2}{6}$ for rectangle), horizontal and vertical shear stress ($\tau = \frac{VQ}{Ib}$), beam deflection equations, column Euler buckling load ($P_{cr} = \frac{\pi^2 E I}{(KL)^2}$).
* **STRUC3 (Theory of Structures):** NSCP 2015 factored gravity ultimate load combinations ($W_u = 1.2D + 1.6L$), simply supported uniform beam moment ($M_u = \frac{W_u L^2}{8}$), cantilever fixed-end moment ($M_u = \frac{W_u L^2}{2}$), support shear ($V_u = \frac{W_u L}{2}$), ACI moment coefficients for continuous beams and slabs ($-1/10 w L_n^2$ at first interior support).
* **STRUC4 (Reinforced Concrete Design — RCD):** Singly reinforced rectangular beam flexural design, tension-controlled strength reduction factor ($\phi = 0.90$), Whitney equivalent rectangular stress block depth ($a = \frac{A_s f_y}{0.85 f'_c b}$), nominal flexural strength ($M_n = A_s f_y [d - a/2]$), design moment capacity ($\phi M_n$), minimum and maximum steel reinforcement ratios ($\rho_{min}$ and $\rho_{max}$), minimum clear concrete cover ($75\text{mm}$ earth, $40\text{mm}$ beams/columns, $20\text{mm}$ slabs).
