## 2. Official Program Description (HIGH — verbatim, M-01)

> "The Architecture program is designed to guide students in progressively assimilating the technical aspects of Architecture while developing in them the sensitivity to their rich cultural heritage. The program also aims to develop students into well-rounded individuals prepared to meet the challenges and dynamics of the profession. Toward these ends, the training combines theoretical and practical aspects with emphasis on the optimum development of the students' analytical and creative skills. In addition, they are trained in the use of information technology and electronic media to enhance research and communication capabilities. While emphasis is on the widest possible latitude for individual solutions, team approach to problem-seeking and problem-solving is also encouraged especially in the senior years."

## 3. Program Educational Objectives (HIGH — official, M-01)

Within five years after graduation, graduates shall have:
1. Mastery of comprehensive architectural knowledge (theory and practice) and proficiency in technical skills necessary for the **global practice of architecture**.
2. A high standard of **professional ethics**, values, attitudes and sense of responsibility.
3. A keen sense of **history and culture** in line with preservation of the architectural heritage of the country.
4. Designed the built environment in the context of **ecological balance and sustainable development**.
5. Become architectural practitioners who can initiate and conduct **architectural research and development** for the advancement of the profession.

## 4. Program Outcomes (HIGH — official, M-01, A–M)

By graduation, students can:
- **A.** produce and present architectural solutions applying knowledge in history, theory, building technology and utilities, structural concepts and professional practice;
- **B.** produce and present planning solutions in the context of ecological balance and sustainable development;
- **C.** apply concepts from allied disciplines into various design projects;
- **D.** prepare contract documents, technical reports and other legal documents;
- **E.** apply concepts, principles, methods and laws regarding conservation of historical and cultural heritage;
- **F.** interpret and apply architectural and planning laws, rules, regulations and standards of professional practice;
- **G.** apply research or appropriate data-gathering methodology;
- **H.** apply ICT media for architectural solutions and presentations;
- **I.** function in multidisciplinary teams;
- **J.** understand professional and ethical responsibility;
- **K.** communicate effectively;
- **L.** recognize the need for and engage in lifelong learning;
- **M.** know contemporary issues.

## 5. CURRICULUM VERSION & CONFIDENCE

The public record shows **three eras** of Mapúa BS Architecture curriculum organization. The current version cannot be fully confirmed from public sources; course-level detail requires authenticated MyMapúa access (see §6).

| # | Era | Academic calendar | Course-code style | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pre-2020 (MIT era)** | Quarterly system (4 terms/yr); program advertised ~13 quarters | Codes such as AR120–AR132; "AR200 = Architectural Design 9 – Thesis Research Writing"; sections like "AR200/4TH/E01"; school named "School of AR-ID-BE" | CourseHero student pages (SY 2015–2020); student portfolio (SY 2017–18); finduniversity listing ("13 quarters") | MEDIUM (secondary, consistent) |
| 2 | **~2020–2021+ (trimestral transition)** | Shift to trimester system; optional summer | "AR1xx" codes with suffixes: **-1** (term-sectioned), **-1P** (paired), **P** (paired/practicum), **S** (studio-style/seminar); e.g., AR142-1P Design 2, AR145-1S Design 5, AR113S VisCom 3, AR200-2 Thesis | Scribd syllabus AR142-1P (2021); Studocu docs (2021–2026); AR113S paper dated "1st Trimester 2025–2026" | MEDIUM–HIGH for code existence (multiple independent student uploads) |
| 3 | **AY 2024–2025 current** | Trimester confirmed officially (14 weeks × 3; summer ≤6 units) | Same AR1xx suffix system observed in 2023–2026 uploads | Academic Handbook AY 2024–25; 2025–26 student documents | HIGH for calendar; MEDIUM for full course list |

**Conclusion (stated explicitly):** The *official* AY 2024–25 handbook confirms the program, outcomes, and trimester system but does **not** publish the full course-by-course curriculum. The complete official curriculum (exact current course codes, units, prerequisites, sequence) is **not publicly established** from Tier-1/2 sources. Course-level codes in this package come from student-uploaded documents (Tier 4) and are labeled accordingly.

**Known code conflicts between eras (must not be silently merged):**
- **AR124**: History of Architecture 4 (Studocu, current-era listing) **vs** Theory of Architecture 2 (CourseHero, SY 2019–20). Recorded as a conflict; treat AR124 as historically used for different courses in different versions.
- **AR130P**: Theory of Architecture 1 (Studocu, current) **vs** "AR 130P THEORY OF ARCHITECTURE 1" (CourseHero, same code confirmed) — consistent, HIGH.
- **AR132**: Theory of Architecture 2 (Studocu AR132-1; CourseHero AR132) — consistent across eras, HIGH.
- **AR200**: "Architectural Design 9 – Thesis Research Writing" (SY 2017–18) and "terminal design course AR200-2" (thesis template, ~2022) — the code persisted while the numbering of the design series changed.
- **Institution mixing hazard:** MCM Davao's AR-2020 curriculum (AR110P Design 1, AR151 HoA 2, AR152 HoA 3, AR130 Building Technology 1, AR136-1 Steel & Timber Design, etc.) uses a **different numbering** and must never be cited as Mapúa University data.

## 7. National Regulatory Framework (Tier 3)

- **CHED CMO No. 61, s. 2017** — "Policies, Standards and Guidelines for the Bachelor of Science in Architecture (BS Archi)" is the governing national standard (confirmed via CHED's official CMO registry and a peer-published policy analysis). It defines minimum program outcomes, competencies, and allows HEI flexibility in curriculum design.
- **CHED Sample Curriculum for BS Architecture (2017)** — official sample (218 credit units total; ≥44 professional courses/115 units; 3 specialization courses/9 units; 3-unit correlation course in the final year; mathematics 13 units; natural sciences 9 units; basic engineering 18 units). Mapúa's curriculum is its own implementation of these standards; the sample is used in this package only as the national reference template (G-03).
- **RA 9266 (Architecture Act of 2004)** — regulates registration, licensing, and practice of architecture; repealed RA 545. Relevant for professional-practice coursework (see PROFESSIONAL_PRACTICE.md and LAWS_CODES_STANDARDS.md).
- **PRC / Professional Regulatory Board of Architecture (PRBoA)** — administers the Licensure Examination for Architects (ALE).
