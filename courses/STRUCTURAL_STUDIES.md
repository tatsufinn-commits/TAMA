## 2. Knowledge domains per course (reviewer-oriented, architectural emphasis)

### Statics (MEC30)
- Resultants & equilibrium of force systems; free-body diagrams; centroids; moments of inertia; friction concepts.
- **Architectural framing:** identify load paths; understand beam/column/truss behavior qualitatively; supports & reactions.

### Strength of Materials (MEC32)
- Stress & strain (axial, shear, bearing, thermal); elasticity & Hooke's law; torsion basics; bending: flexure formula (σ = Mc/I), shear in beams (τ = VQ/Ib); beam deflection concepts; combined stresses; columns basics (Euler concepts).
- **Architectural emphasis:** size beam/column intuition, material behavior (steel vs concrete vs timber), deflection/serviceability awareness.

### Theory of Structures (CE131P)
- Determinate vs indeterminate structures; beams, frames, trusses; method of joints/sections; influence lines concepts; moment distribution / slope-deflection (intro); structural idealization.
- **Architectural emphasis:** structural systems selection for building types; grid, frame, shear-wall, and moment-frame behavior; stability.

### Steel & Timber Design (CE134P)
- Steel: AISC/NSCP-based design basics — tension, compression, bending members; connections intro; composite behavior.
- Timber: timber properties, allowable-stress design, simple beams/columns/connections; Philippine timber species (potential).
- **NSCP (National Structural Code of the Philippines)** is the governing reference — architects must know its role.

### Reinforced Concrete Design (CE133/CE191)
- Concrete & steel properties; design of beams (flexure), slabs (one-way/two-way concepts), columns (short), footings basics; development length; detailing & bar schedules (architect-coordination value).
- **Architectural emphasis:** column grid economy, beam depths, slab spans, structural layout for plan flexibility.

### Surveying (CE120)
- Linear measurement, leveling, angles, traverse computations; topographic surveys; introduction to GPS/total stations.
- **Architectural use:** site documentation, contours, lot surveys — feeds Planning 1 (site planning) per template.

### Architectural Structures (template, 5u) [CHED]
- Comprehensive structural concepts for architecture: systems (skeletal, surface, membrane, shell, tensile, pneumatic), lateral loads (wind/seismic — important in the Philippines), load combinations, structural coordination in design; seismic design concepts (NSCP earthquake provisions).
