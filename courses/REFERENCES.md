## Source hierarchy application notes
1. Tier 1 (official handbook) outranks everything for program-level facts.
2. Tier 3 (CHED sample curriculum) is used as the national template and is **always labeled** — never presented as Mapúa's curriculum.
3. Tier 4 (Studocu/CourseHero/Scribd/portfolios) is the only public source of Mapúa course codes; codes are treated as "observed," with conflicts recorded rather than resolved silently.
4. Community forums (Reddit) contribute culture/context only.
5. Where editions of technical codes (PEC/PMC/PNPC/NSCP/Green Code) could not be verified, documents flag "verify current."

All sources retrieved 2026-08-10. Product/course data may change; re-verify Tier-1 handbook and CHED issuances for any later revision of this research.
