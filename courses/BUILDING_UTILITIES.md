## 3. Knowledge domains per course (reviewer-oriented)

### BU1 – Plumbing & Sanitary [CHED + standard practice]
- Water supply systems: sources, storage, distribution (upfeed/downfeed), pipe sizing concepts, fixtures & fittings, water demand estimation.
- Sanitary drainage: soil/waste/vent systems, traps, cleanouts, septic systems, sewage disposal, storm drainage.
- **Revised National Plumbing Code of the Philippines (PNPC)** — the governing code (RA 1378-era; current edition 2018+ — verify).
- Materials: PVC, PPR, copper, GI; corrosion; water hammer; backflow prevention.
- Green water design: rainwater harvesting, greywater (observed relevance in Mapúa student content).
- Plumbing symbols and drawings; coordination with architectural drawings.

### BU2 – Electrical, Electronics & Mechanical [CHED]
- **Philippine Electrical Code (PEC)** fundamentals: branch circuits, feeders, load computation, service entrance, panelboards, lighting outlets, grounding; electrical plans and symbols.
- Electronics/communications: telephone/data, fire alarm, security, sound systems, low-voltage systems, ICT infrastructure (RA 10934? — **verify**; standard: National Building Code provisions).
- Mechanical: **Philippine Mechanical Code** — HVAC concepts, air conditioning load basics, ventilation, elevators/escalators (vertical transportation), mechanical rooms, ductwork/risers; fire protection pumps.

### BU3 – Acoustics & Lighting [CHED]
- **Lighting:** lighting quantities (lumens, lux, foot-candles), color temperature, CRI, lamps (incandescent, fluorescent, LED), luminaries, lighting design methods (zonal cavity), daylighting, lighting standards (IES; Philippine practice), controls.
- **Acoustics:** sound physics (dB, dBA), reverberation time, absorption/reflection/diffusion, sound transmission class (STC), impact insulation class (IIC), room acoustics design, noise control, acoustic materials, electro-acoustic systems basics.
- Coordination with electrical design; architectural integration.

### Fire Protection (PFC-01 + BU integration) [OBS + national code]
- **RA 9514 (Fire Code of the Philippines)** — the 2008 Fire Code (repealed PD 1185; **verify current**).
- Fire-resistive construction, occupancy classifications, means of egress, fire/smoke detection, alarm systems, suppression (standpipes, sprinklers — NFPA-based), Fire Safety Evaluation, fire exit requirements, building permit requirements re BFP (Bureau of Fire Protection).
