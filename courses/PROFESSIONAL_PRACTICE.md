## 2. RA 9266 — The Architecture Act of 2004 (verified via lawphil.net and SC e-Library) [LAW — HIGH]

Key provisions relevant to professional practice coursework:
- **Purpose:** more responsive regulation of registration, licensing, and practice of architecture; repealed RA 545.
- **Definition of practice:** "planning and architectural designing, structural conceptualization, specifying, supervising, giving general administration and responsible direction to the erection, enlargement, or alterations of buildings."
- **Scope of practice:** professional services in connection with site, physical planning, and design/construction/enlargement/conservation/renovation/remodeling/restoration/alteration of buildings or groups of buildings.
- **Structural conceptualization:** "the act of conceiving, choosing, developing the type, disposition, arrangement and proportioning of the structural elements of an architectural work" — i.e., within the architect's scope.
- **Registration required:** no person may practice architecture, prepare architectural plans/specs/preliminary data, or use the title "Architect" without PRC registration + Professional Identification Card.
- **Architect-in-charge of construction:** directly and professionally responsible/liable for construction supervision.
- **Consulting Architect:** academically/professionally qualified with recognized expertise/specialization.
- **Architectural firm:** sole proprietorship, partnership, or corporation registered with DTI/SEC and with the Board of Architecture and PRC.
- **Board:** Professional Regulatory Board of Architecture (PRBoA) under PRC.

## 3. Knowledge domains per course (reviewer-oriented)

### PP1 – Laws Affecting the Practice of Architecture [CHED + LAW]
- RA 9266 and its IRR (2005); PRBoA functions; registration process; CPD (RA 10912 — **verify**).
- **National Building Code (PD 1096)** & IRR; BP 344 (Accessibility Law); Fire Code (RA 9514); Revised Plumbing Code; Philippine Electrical/Mechanical Codes; zoning/local ordinances; RA 7160 permitting.
- Professional organizations: UAP (United Architects of the Philippines — the integrated/accredited professional organization referenced in RA 9266), APEC Architects/ASEAN Architect frameworks (global practice links).
- Ethics: Code of Ethical Conduct (UAP/PRBoA), Architects' National Code; liability basics.

### PP2 – Administering the Regular Services of the Architect [CHED]
- Phases of architectural services (pre-design, schematic, design development, contract documents, bidding/negotiation, construction administration, post-occupancy) — per UAP/standard documents.
- Owner-architect agreements; scope of services; fee structures (percentage of construction cost, lump sum, per-hour — UAP schedule of fees as reference).
- Contract documents: drawings, specifications, general conditions; bid documents.
- Construction administration: RFIs, shop drawings, change orders, payment certification, ASI (Architect's Supplemental Instructions), site observation reports.
- Project delivery methods (design-bid-build, design-build, CM).

### PP3 – Global Practice [CHED]
- International practice: ASEAN Architect, APEC Architect, cross-border practice rules (RA 9266 foreign-architect provisions), global professional standards.
- Cultural/regulatory differences; global sustainability frameworks (LEED, BERDE, EDGE).

### PP4 / Business Management (AR188) [CHED outcomes + OBS]
- Office administration; business planning; fee negotiation; marketing; project accounting; legal forms of practice (sole prop/partnership/corporation); insurance; professional liability.
- Template outcome: "how to sustain an architectural practice; single proprietorship, partnership, corporate practice."

### Research & Writing courses (adjacent) [OBS]
- AR134-1 Research Method for Architecture: research design, data gathering (Program Outcome G), literature review, thesis methodology.
- AR135-1 Architectural Essays & Reviews: critical writing, reviews of architectural works/literature.

## 6. Licensure pathway (ALE) context [PRC — secondary, MEDIUM]
The Licensure Examination for Architects covers: (1) History & Theory of Architecture, Principles of Planning, Architectural Practice (~30%); (2) Structural Design, Building Materials & Construction, Utilities (~30%); (3) Architectural Design & Site Planning incl. urban design and interiors (~40%). Passing: weighted general average ≥70%, no subject <50%. (Sources: reviewersph, 3d-universal, open-exam-prep — secondary; verify against current PRBoA resolutions.)
