## 2. History-of-architecture knowledge domains (reviewer-oriented)

Period → Context → Characteristics → Architects → Works → Influence. For each period, reviewer content should cover:
- **Prehistoric:** megaliths (dolmen, menhir, cromlech), cave dwellings, first shelters — observed at Mapúa.
- **Mesopotamian:** ziggurats, mud-brick construction, city-states — observed at Mapúa.
- **Egyptian:** mastabas, pyramids (step → true), temples (hypostyle hall), post-and-lintel — observed.
- **Aegean/Greek:** Minoan/Mycenaean; Greek orders (Doric/Ionic/Corinthian), temple anatomy, Acropolis — observed (Greek temple design doc).
- **Roman:** arch, vault, dome, concrete, forums, basilicas, aqueducts — potential (standard).
- **Early Christian & Byzantine:** basilican plan, central plan, mosaics, pendentives — observed (Byzantine doc).
- **Romanesque:** barrel/groin vaults, pilgrimage churches, monasteries — potential.
- **Gothic:** pointed arch, rib vault, flying buttress, stained glass — potential.
- **Renaissance:** Brunelleschi, Alberti, Palladio; revival of classical orders; dome of Florence — observed (Renaissance doc).
- **Baroque/Rococo:** drama, movement, urbanism (Bernini, Borromini) — potential.
- **18th–19th century revivals:** Neoclassicism, Gothic Revival, Beaux-Arts; Industrial Revolution materials (cast iron, steel, glass) — observed (AR123 doc).
- **Modern movements:** Arts & Crafts, Art Nouveau, Bauhaus, International Style, organic architecture, postmodernism — observed partially (Bauhaus summary paper under TOA at Mapúa; AR132 postmodern content).
- **Philippine architecture:** bahay kubo, bahay na bato, Spanish colonial churches, American-era civic buildings, notable Filipino architects (National Artists) — observed (Filipino architects doc).
- **Vernacular & regional:** Southeast Asian vernacular houses — observed (AR123 doc).
- **Islamic:** mosque typology, muqarnas, horseshoe arch — observed (AR123 quiz content).

**Connection format for reviewers:** Period → Context → Characteristics → Architects → Works → Influence (per directive).

## 4. Theory knowledge domains (reviewer-oriented)

- **Definitions & etymology** of architecture (architekton; art and science of building).
- **Elements of architecture:** space, form, mass, plane, line, point; ordering principles (axis, symmetry, hierarchy, rhythm, datum, transformation).
- **Form & space relationships:** solid/void, enclosure (base plane, vertical planes, overhead plane), light and view.
- **Proportion & scale:** human scale, proportional systems (golden section, modular, classical orders).
- **Perception & behavior:** proxemics (E.T. Hall), territoriality, personal space; Gestalt principles (potential).
- **Design process & programming:** problem seeking, concept development, parti, schematics (observed "architectural programming notes").
- **Theories & theorists:** functionalism; formalism; structuralism/post-structuralism; semiotics/semiology (observed — Winland Klassen semiological approach); phenomenology (potential); regionalism (potential); sustainability theory (observed essays).
- **Movements & styles:** Bauhaus, International Style, postmodernism (observed); deconstructivism, parametricism (potential).
- **Critical writing:** essays/reviews (AR135-1 Architectural Essays & Reviews supports this — see index).
