## 5. Professional standards & organizations
- **UAP (United Architects of the Philippines)** — the integrated and accredited professional organization under RA 9266; Code of Ethical Conduct; Documents (agreements, schedules of fees).
- **PRBoA (Professional Regulatory Board of Architecture)** — licensure (ALE), registration.
- **ASEAN Architect / APEC Architect** — regional/global mobility frameworks (PP3 relevance).
- **BERDE** (PH Green Building Council), **LEED** (USGBC) — green certification references.
