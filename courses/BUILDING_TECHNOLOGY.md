## 2. Knowledge domains per course (reviewer-oriented)

### BT1 – Building Materials [CHED + standard practice]
- Classification of building materials (stone, ceramics, timber, metals, concrete, glass, polymers, composites).
- Properties: physical, mechanical, thermal, acoustic, durability; sustainable material selection (Mapúa PEO emphasis on sustainability).
- Philippine context: indigenous materials, climate-responsive selection (tropical climate), locally available products.
- **Relevant standards:** ASTM/ASTM-International and Philippine National Standards (PNS) references; product data sheets.

### BT2 – Construction Drawings (1-storey) [CHED]
- Working drawings: floor plans, elevations, sections, details.
- Construction systems in wood, steel, and concrete for small buildings.
- Foundations (shallow types), wall systems, roof framing (wood trusses, steel), openings.
- Drawing conventions, line weights, dimensioning, title blocks; scale and notation.

### BT3 – Construction Drawings (2-storey residential) [CHED]
- Multi-level construction: stairs, floor systems, structural grid coordination.
- Integration of utilities into drawings.
- More complex detailing: waterproofing, egress, building envelope.

### BT4 – Specification Writing & Quantity Surveying [CHED]
- Types of specifications (proprietary, performance, descriptive); CSI/MasterFormat-style organization (international practice; Philippine adaptation common).
- Quantity take-offs; estimating; bill of materials; cost documentation.
- Relation to contract documents (template outcome: "prepare contract documents").

### BT5 – Alternative Building Construction Systems [CHED]
- Prefabrication, modular construction, steel frame systems, tilt-up, insulated concrete forms, bamboo/alternative materials (PH context).
- Green building systems: LEED/USGBC (MCL campus offers LEED credential — separate institution, but indicative of group direction), BERDE (Philippine Green Building Council) as relevant national standard (potential).

### VisCom / Graphic Design / CAD / BIM [OBS + CHED]
- AR101P Graphic Design: visual fundamentals, composition, color, lettering, graphic presentation.
- AR113S VisCom 3: observed student work = digital architecture research; course covers visual communication techniques (potential: rendering, presentation boards).
- AR114S-1 VisCom 4: advanced presentation (potential).
- CAD10L/30L: AutoCAD-based drafting (title as uploaded).
- AR117-1P BIM 1: BIM concepts, Revit workflow (observed course exists; tools inferred from group-wide practice).
