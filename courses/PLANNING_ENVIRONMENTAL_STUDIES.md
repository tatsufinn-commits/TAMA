## 2. Knowledge domains per course (reviewer-oriented)

### Planning 1 – Site Planning & Landscape Architecture [CHED + standard]
- Site analysis: natural (topography, drainage, soil, vegetation, climate/microclimate) and man-made (utilities, access, context, zoning).
- Site development: building placement/orientation, circulation (vehicular/pedestrian), parking standards, setbacks, easements.
- Landscape architecture basics: site grading & drainage design concepts, planting design, landscape materials, site furniture, landscape plans.
- Zoning & land-use concepts; local government (HLURB — now DHSUD — standards, **verify current agency**).
- Philippine context: tropical climate response, flood-prone area considerations.

### Planning 2 – Urban Design & Community Architecture [CHED]
- Urban design fundamentals: urban form, block/street/parcel systems, public spaces, pedestrian environments, density and mixed use.
- Community architecture: participatory design, neighborhood units, community facilities, housing clusters, site planning at community scale.
- Urban design precedents; Philippine urban issues (informal settlements, urban sprawl).

### Planning 3 – Urban & Regional Planning [CHED]
- Planning theory and processes; comprehensive land-use plans; regional planning concepts; transport planning basics; environmental planning (EIA concepts); urban economics; planning legislation.
- **Key Philippine instruments:** CLUP (Comprehensive Land Use Plan), Zoning Ordinance, CDP (Comprehensive Development Plan), HLURB/DHSUD guidelines (verify), NEDA regional plans, RA 10587 (Environmental Planning Act of 2013 — **verify**; regulates environmental planners), PD 1308-era planning regulation.
- Local Government Code (RA 7160) planning powers.

### Tropical Design [CHED + PH context]
- Tropical climate: sun path, solar geometry, wind patterns, humidity, rainfall, typhoons.
- Passive cooling: shading devices, cross-ventilation, stack effect, thermal mass vs lightweight construction, roof forms, building orientation, overhangs, louvers.
- Climate-responsive design strategies; bioclimatic chart use.
- Philippine vernacular responses (bahay kubo raised floor, steep roofs, permeable walls) as precedents.
- Sustainable/energy-efficient design links (Mapúa PEO: ecological balance).

### Housing (template) [CHED]
- Housing needs & policy (PH: RA 7279 Urban Development & Housing Act, NHA, DHSUD), housing types (single/multi-family, walk-up, high-rise), density standards, site planning for housing, socialized housing provisions, cost-economics.
