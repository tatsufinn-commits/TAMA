# 2. PRC ARCHITECTURE LICENSURE EXAM (ALE) 3-PART BLUEPRINT

Mapúa Comprehensive Exit Exams are modeled directly on the **PRC Board of Architecture Licensure Examination**:

### Part 1: History, Theory, Planning & Building Laws (30% Weight)
* **History of Architecture (HOA):** Ancient Egyptian, Classical Greek/Roman, Byzantine, Gothic, Renaissance, Asian (Chinese/Japanese/Islamic), Philippine Vernacular & Spanish Colonial, Modern Movements.
* **Theory of Architecture (TOA):** Form, Space, and Order (Francis D.K. Ching), Anthropometrics, Architectural Psychology.
* **Building Laws (The Core 4):**
  1. **PD 1096 (National Building Code of the Philippines — NBCP):** Rules 7 & 8 (AMBF, TGFA, Setbacks, Parking, Light & Ventilation).
  2. **RA 9514 (Revised Fire Code of the Philippines):** Means of egress, occupant loads, stair dimensions, fire sprinkler triggers.
  3. **BP 344 (Accessibility Law):** PWD ramps (1:12 slope), tactile paving, handrails, accessible toilet stalls.
  4. **RA 9266 (The Architecture Act of 2004):** Scope of practice, seal ownership, criminal liabilities, SPP Document 200 series.

### Part 2: Building Tech, Utilities, Materials & Structures (30% Weight)
* **Building Materials & Construction (BT1–BT5):** Concrete mixtures, timber defects, steel shapes, joinery, curtain walls, waterproofing.
* **Building Utilities (BU1–BU4):** Plumbing drainage systems, electrical circuit breakers, HVAC refrigeration cycles, acoustic RT60 reverberation calculations.
* **Structural Design (MEC30–CE134P):** Force equilibrium, shear/moment diagrams, bending stress, column buckling, NSCP seismic load factors.

### Part 3: Architectural Design & Site Planning (40% Weight)
* Situational design problems requiring simultaneous application of zoning regulations, parking counts, AMBF computation, and space planning layouts.

# 3. CRITICAL MAPÚA EXAM CALCULATION FORMULAS

### 1. Allowable Maximum Building Footprint (AMBF):
$$\text{AMBF} = \text{Total Lot Area (TLA)} - \text{Total Open Space within Lot (TOSL)}$$
$$\text{TOSL} = \text{Unpaved Open Space (USA)} + \text{Impervious Open Space (ISA)}$$

### 2. Maximum Allowable Percentage of Site Occupancy (PSO):
$$\text{PSO} = \frac{\text{AMBF}}{\text{TLA}} \times 100\% \quad (\text{Per NBCP Table VII.1})$$

### 3. Total Gross Floor Area (TGFA):
$$\text{TGFA} = \text{TLA} \times \text{Floor to Lot Area Ratio (FLAR)} \quad (\text{Per NBCP Table VII.G.1})$$

### 4. Continuous Beam Maximum Moment ($wL^2/8$ vs Fixed End Moments):
$$M_u = \frac{W_u \cdot L^2}{8} \quad (\text{Simply Supported Uniform Load})$$
$$W_u = 1.2D + 1.6L \quad (\text{NSCP Factored Ultimate Load Combination})$$
