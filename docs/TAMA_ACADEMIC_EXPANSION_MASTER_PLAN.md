## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Diagnosis

### 1.1 The Problem: The "Meta-Scaffolding" Trap
An audit of previous agent contributions reveals that substantial effort was directed toward **administrative meta-scaffolding**:
- Creating governance documents, versioning guides, decision-authority matrices, debt registers, and test scripts (`audit.js`, `knowledge-regression.js`, `test-scaffold.js`).
- Ingesting surface-level markdown files containing brief, disconnected bullet points (Level 1–2 flashcard snippets like *"ramp slope = 1:12"* or *"pre-tensioning happens before pour"*).
- Creating mock exam items that test rote memorization rather than situational design synthesis.

### 1.2 The Core Academic Deficiency
All previous iterations missed the **context, depth, and academic relevance** of actual architectural coursework:
1. **Isolated Course Silos**: Courses were treated as independent trivia banks rather than an interconnected academic engine feeding into the **Design Studio Spine (AR141 ➔ AR200-2)**.
2. **Absence of Pedagogical Progression**: Content lacked Bloom's Taxonomy progression (Foundations ➔ Technical Assemblies ➔ Statutory Derivations ➔ Calculations ➔ Studio Plate Application).
3. **Superficiality in Technical & Structural Topics**: Structural analysis, MEPFS engineering, and building assemblies were reduced to summary lists without structural mechanics, load paths, sizing heuristics, or construction detailing.
4. **Weak Statutory Grounding**: Building laws lacked exact statutory cross-referencing (e.g. failing to cite specific section numbers, tables, or exception clauses in PD 1096, RA 9514, and BP 344).

### 1.3 Strategic Mission
Transform the entire TAMA knowledge vault into a university-grade, high-yield academic repository that equips the student to excel in **Mapúa 3rd-Year Departmentals, Design Plate Defenses, Comprehensive Exit Exams, and the PRC Architecture Licensure Examination (ALE)**.

### Detailed Cluster Breakdown:

### Cluster 1: Building Technology Series (BT1 – BT5)
- **BT1 (AR161/163-1):** Building Materials & Wood Construction — Timber species (Philippine hardwoods: Narra, Yakal, Guijo, Molave), wood seasoning/defects, joinery details, masonry mortar/grout mixes, ASTM standards.
- **BT2 (AR162-1P):** Construction Drawings & 1-Storey Framing — Shallow footings, slab-on-grade, wall framing, roof trusses, architectural working drawings conventions, line weights, dimensioning strings.
- **BT3 (AR164-1):** Heavy Construction & 2-Storey RCD/Steel — Reinforced concrete column/beam formwork, scaffolding, rebar bending schedules, steel truss connections, waterproofing membranes, envelope insulation.
- **BT4:** Specifications Writing & Quantity Surveying — CSI MasterFormat (50 Divisions), 3-part specification structure (General, Products, Execution), unit cost estimation, Bill of Quantities (BOQ), material take-offs.
- **BT5 (AR165-1P):** Advanced Building Construction Systems — Unitized vs. stick curtain walls, structural silicone glazing, post-tensioned flat plates, precast sandwich panels, space frames, seismic expansion joints.

### Cluster 2: Structural Studies Series (MEC30 ➔ CE133/191)
- **MEC30 (Statics):** Force equilibrium, coplanar/non-coplanar force systems, free-body diagrams, centroid and moment of inertia derivations, determinate truss analysis (Method of Joints & Sections).
- **MEC32 (Strength of Materials):** Direct stress/strain, Hooke's Law, thermal stress, shear and moment equations, flexure formula ($\sigma = My/I$), horizontal shear stress ($\tau = VQ/Ib$), beam deflection, Euler column buckling.
- **CE131P (Theory of Structures):** Indeterminate structural analysis, Slope-Deflection Method, Moment Distribution Method (Hardy Cross) for non-sway and sidesway multi-bay frames, structural idealization.
- **CE133 / CE191 (Reinforced Concrete Design):** Ultimate Strength Design (USD) per NSCP 2015 / ACI 318, Whitney stress block ($a = \beta_1 c$), singly/doubly reinforced rectangular beams, T-beams, shear reinforcement ($V_c + V_s$), rebar development length, column interaction diagrams ($P_n - M_n$).
- **CE134P (Steel & Timber Design):** AISC/NSCP Load and Resistance Factor Design (LRFD) vs. ASD, tension members (net effective area $A_e = U A_n$), compression members ($KL/r$), lateral-torsional buckling in W-beams, bolted/welded connections.

### Cluster 3: Building Utilities Series (BU1 – BU4 / MEPFS)
- **BU1 (AR153P - Plumbing & Sanitary):** Water supply networks (direct upfeed vs downfeed hydro-pneumatic), Fixture Unit (FU) demand calculations, drainage waste & vent (DWV) sizing per Revised National Plumbing Code of the Philippines, interceptors, septic tank sizing (3-chamber digestion/leaching), storm drainage run-off ($Q = C I A$).
- **BU2 (Electrical & Mechanical Systems):** Philippine Electrical Code (PEC) compliance, branch circuit design, total connected load vs demand load ($VA$), voltage drop, circuit breaker/conduit sizing, single-line diagrams, emergency generator sizing.
- **BU3 (AR155-1 - Acoustics & Lighting Systems):** Architectural acoustics, Sabine formula ($RT_{60} = \frac{0.161 V}{A}$), Noise Criterion (NC) curves, STC/IIC floor-wall assemblies, daylighting factor ($DF$), lumen method lighting calculations ($E = \frac{N \times \Phi \times CU \times LLF}{A}$), CRI, CCT, glare ratings.
- **BU4 / Central MEPFS (HVAC, Vertical Transport, Fire Protection):** Refrigeration cycle, Variable Refrigerant Flow (VRF) vs. Chilled Water Central Plants, Air Handling Units (AHU) and dedicated outdoor air systems (DOAS), duct sizing (equal friction method), elevator round-trip time (RTT) and handling capacity ($HC\%$), NFPA 13 / RA 9514 wet-pipe sprinkler hydraulic calculations.

### Cluster 4: Design Studio Spine (AR141 – AR200-2)
- **Space Programming & Behavioral Mapping:** Net-to-Gross Area ratios (NFA ➔ GFA ➔ TGFA), functional adjacency matrices, bubble/relationship diagrams, activity workflow circuits.
- **Site Planning & Tropical Microclimates:** Passive cooling strategies, solar sun-path analysis (solar azimuth/altitude charts, horizontal/vertical sun-shading device design), wind-driven ventilation (*Amihan* & *Habagat* stack effect), slope grading (cut-and-fill balance, storm detention ponds).
- **Comprehensive Plate Defense Protocols:** Juried defense frameworks for 3rd-year institutional/commercial complexes, barrier-free design integration (BP 344), fire egress topology (RA 9514), MEPFS shaft distribution, and structural grid coordination.

### Cluster 5: History & Theory of Architecture (HOA 1–4 & TOA 1–2)
- **HOA 1–4:** Chronological and critical historiography: Ancient Egyptian/Mesopotamian, Classical Greek/Roman orders, Early Christian/Byzantine, Romanesque, High Gothic structural logic, Renaissance humanism, Baroque dynamic spatiality, 19th-Century Industrial/Eclecticism, Modernism (Bauhaus, International Style, Brutalism), Post-Modernism, Deconstructivism, and Philippine Vernacular/Colonial/Post-War Architecture.
- **TOA 1–2:** Formal, spatial, and organizational theory: Francis D.K. Ching's *Form, Space, and Order*, spatial articulation, proportioning systems (Golden Ratio, Modulor, Ken), phenomenological architecture, Critical Regionalism, architectural semiotics, and environmental psychology.

### Cluster 6: Planning, Urban Design & Environmental Studies (PLAN 1–3)
- **Site Planning (PLAN1):** Environmental site capacity, ecological land-use planning, slope analysis ($>18\%$ slope thresholds), soil mechanics for site development, watershed hydrology.
- **Urban Design & Housing (PLAN2):** Kevin Lynch's urban elements (Paths, Edges, Districts, Nodes, Landmarks), TOD (Transit-Oriented Development), BP 220 (Socialized/Economic Housing) vs. PD 957 (Open Market/Medium Cost Subdivisions) lot dimensions, setbacks, community facilities allocation.
- **Comprehensive Land Use Planning (PLAN3 / CLUP):** Zoning ordinances, socio-economic and demographic analysis, land suitability analysis, disaster risk reduction and climate change adaptation (DRRM-CCA).

### Cluster 7: Professional Practice & Building Laws
- **The Core 4 Philippine Building Laws:**
  1. **PD 1096 (National Building Code of the Philippines):** Full Rule 7 & 8 computation mechanics (TLA, TOSL, USA, ISA, AMBF, PSO, BHL, FLAR, TGFA), occupancy classifications (Groups A–J), fire zones, light & ventilation court requirements.
  2. **RA 9514 (Revised Fire Code of the Philippines):** Occupant load factors per occupancy, exit capacity width factors ($7.6\text{ mm/person}$ for stairs, $5.0\text{ mm/person}$ for doors/corridors), travel distance limits (sprinklered vs non-sprinklered), common path of travel, dead-end corridors, fire barrier ratings.
  3. **BP 344 (Accessibility Law):** Anthropometrics of PWDs, ramp slopes ($1:12$ maximum gradient, $6.00\text{m}$ maximum run between landings), door widths ($0.80\text{m}$ clear), accessible toilet layouts, tactile ground surface indicators (TGSI).
  4. **RA 9266 (The Architecture Act of 2004) & SPP 200 Series:** Scope of exclusive architectural practice, ownership of architectural plans/documents, liability for structural collapse (Art. 1723 Civil Code vs RA 9266), UAP-IAPOA Standard of Professional Practice (SPP 201 Pre-Design to SPP 208 Architectural Design Competition), UAP Doc 301 General Conditions of Contract (Bidding, Contract Documents, Payments, Retainage, Penalties).
