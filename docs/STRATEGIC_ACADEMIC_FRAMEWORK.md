# 1. THE 4-AXIS EXAM YIELD INDEX (EYI SCORING)

To eliminate study fatigue and ensure you focus on what actually appears on **Mapúa Departmentals and Comprehensive Exit Exams**, every topic is scored across **4 Strategic Dimensions** (1 to 10):

```text
 ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │               THE 4-AXIS TAMA EXAM YIELD MATRIX (EYI)                      │
 ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │ 1. MAPÚA DEPARTMENTAL WEIGHT (DW) [1 = Rarely Tested ➔ 10 = Tested Every Term]
 │ 2. EXIT EXAM & ALE BOARD WEIGHT (EW)[1 = Negligible  ➔ 10 = High Board Weight]
 │ 3. DIFFICULTY & COMPLEXITY (DC)   [1 = Simple Memorize➔ 10 = Hard Calculation]
 │ 4. PERSONAL STUDENT WEAKNESS (SW) [1 = Mastered      ➔ 10 = Severe Weak Spot]
 └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Exam Yield Index (EYI) Formula:
$$\text{EYI} = \frac{(\text{Departmental Weight} \times 3) + (\text{Exit Exam Weight} \times 3) + (\text{Difficulty} \times 2) + (\text{Student Weakness} \times 2)}{10}$$

* **EYI 8.5 – 10.0:** 🔴 **TIER 1 (CRITICAL HIGH-YIELD)** — Master immediately. Tested heavily; causes highest failure rates.
* **EYI 6.5 – 8.4:** 🟡 **TIER 2 (CORE COMPETENCY)** — Standard building tech, MEPFS utilities, and history essentials.
* **EYI < 6.5:** 🟢 **TIER 3 (MAINTENANCE / QUICK REVIEW)** — Obscure history dates, simple definitions. Review in 5-minute flashcard blocks.
